# Jack-in-the-box Oil

Original Trackstorm geometry and packed procedural patina authored in Blender 5.2.2 LTS, guided by user-approved TS-245 Jira attachments 10150 (closed), 10151 (deployed), and 10149 (firing). Reference pixels are not runtime textures or redistributed assets. The existing vehicle, rack, trunk and hydraulics are unchanged. No third-party asset was acquired.

Run `source/BuildJackInTheBox.py` with the installed Blender 5.2 executable in background mode to rebuild `source/JackInTheBox.blend` and `JackInTheBox.glb`. The master retains editable individual panels, diamond paint, crest, straps/rivets, face/hat/teeth/collar and coil. The GLB batches four rigid groups, with separate `LidPivot`, `JesterPivot` and `Spring` parents for runtime articulation. Source is excluded from Godot import. Six colored 256-pixel patina textures are baked into portable Base Color images, packed into the master/GLB and extracted alongside the GLB by Godot. This avoids an unsupported partial Blender MixRGB graph discarding the color tint. Original grayscale noise and color bakes remain in source.

Blender uses metres/Z-up and GLB converts to Godot Y-up. The crate is 0.86 m across including trim. It mounts at rack-local Y=0.09 m; the extended head anchor is Y=1.42 m. Positive Godot Z is rearward. The upper spring endpoint follows the head collar; runtime never generates model collision. `OilJack` instances the cached packed scene through `MatchResourceLoader`; `CarRackPresentation` retains shared mechanism ownership. Core's mouth socket is vehicle-local (0, 2.85, 1.845) plus accepted spring displacement and the tilted local mouth offset (0, 0.025, 0.225). The head's cosmetic tilt does not change authoritative aim.

Black spill meshes and splash/flight parcels are original Godot geometry with wet PBR materials and bounded continuous stream geometry. `OilFluid.gdshader` is original project code, shared through the match resource loader; individual hazard materials retain their own parameters. Geometry and contact share `OilSegment.EdgeRadius`; scene owners free as a unit on authoritative removal. See [held items](../../../docs/features/items.md#persistent-oil) and [Oil tuning](../../../docs/features/developer-options.md#oil-tuning) for lifecycle, controls and limitations.
