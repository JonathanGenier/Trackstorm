# Jack-in-the-box Oil

Original Trackstorm geometry and packed procedural patina authored in Blender 5.2.2 LTS, guided by user-approved TS-245 Jira attachments 10150 (closed), 10151 (deployed), and 10149 (firing). Reference pixels are not runtime textures or redistributed assets. The existing vehicle, rack, trunk and hydraulics are unchanged. No third-party asset was acquired.

Run `source/BuildJackInTheBox.py` with the installed Blender 5.2 executable in background mode to rebuild `source/JackInTheBox.blend` and `JackInTheBox.glb`. The master retains editable individual panels, diamond paint, crest, straps/rivets, face/hat/teeth/collar and coil. The GLB batches four rigid groups, with separate `LidPivot`, `JesterPivot` and `Spring` parents for runtime articulation. Source is excluded from Godot import. Six colored 256-pixel patina textures are baked into portable Base Color images, packed into the master/GLB and extracted alongside the GLB by Godot. This avoids an unsupported partial Blender MixRGB graph discarding the color tint. Original grayscale noise and color bakes remain in source.

Blender uses metres/Z-up and GLB converts to Godot Y-up. The crate remains 0.86 m across including trim; the extended head anchor remains Y=1.42 m. White porcelain, a sculpted upturned grin, continuous red/black tapered hat horns, two gold bells and a thin pleated collar form the jester. Brass straps, hinges and clasp frame white/red decorative panels. Direct colored Base Color bakes preserve these materials in Godot.

The payload mounts at rack-local Y=0.09 m. Positive Godot Z is rearward. An Oil-owned telescoping carriage moves 1.30 m behind the fully raised, unchanged rack before the box opens. The coil begins at box-local Y=0.105 m, seated in the floor, and its 1.145 m authored span ends at the extended collar. The head compresses to 48% scale at Y=0.29 m inside the box during retraction; the closed lid precedes carriage return and rack descent. Runtime never generates model collision. `OilJack` instances the cached packed scene through `MatchResourceLoader`; `CarRackPresentation` retains shared mechanism ownership. Core's mouth socket is vehicle-local (0, 2.85, 3.145) plus accepted spring displacement and the tilted local mouth offset (0, 0.025, 0.225). Neither cosmetic tilt nor recoil turns authoritative aim.

Black spill meshes and splash/flight parcels are original Godot geometry with wet PBR materials and bounded continuous stream geometry. `OilFluid.gdshader` is original project code, shared through the match resource loader; individual hazard materials retain their own parameters. Geometry and contact share `OilSegment.EdgeRadius`; scene owners free as a unit on authoritative removal.
