# Jack-in-the-box Oil

Original Trackstorm geometry and packed procedural patina authored in Blender 5.2.2 LTS, guided by user-approved TS-245 Jira attachments 10150 (closed), 10151 (deployed), 10149 (firing), and 10152 (Laughing oil 2: tank and hoses). Reference pixels are not runtime textures or redistributed assets. The existing vehicle, rack, trunk and hydraulics are unchanged. No third-party asset was acquired.

Run `source/BuildJackInTheBox.py` with the installed Blender 5.2 executable in background mode to rebuild `source/JackInTheBox.blend` and `JackInTheBox.glb`; it executes `source/BuildOilTank.py` for the tank and fittings. The editable master retains individual panels, paint, crest, hardware, face/hat/teeth/collar, coil, tank, gauge and bespoke stroke lettering. The GLB batches rigid groups and retains `LidPivot`, `JesterPivot`, `Spring`, `JawPivot`, `Tank` and `GaugeLiquidLevel` parents. Two separate smile surfaces carry an Open morph; jaw/lower teeth descend while it opens. Source is excluded from Godot import. Six colored 256-pixel patina textures and one 1024×512 tank paint are connected directly to portable Base Color, packed in the master/GLB and extracted beside the GLB by Godot. This avoids the previously unsupported partial MixRGB tint. No external font is used for the lettering.

Blender uses metres/Z-up and GLB converts to Godot Y-up. The crate remains 0.86 m across including trim; the extended head anchor remains Y=1.42 m. White porcelain, a sculpted upturned grin, continuous red/black tapered hat horns, two gold bells and a thin pleated collar form the jester. Brass straps, hinges and clasp frame white/red decorative panels. Direct colored Base Color bakes preserve these materials in Godot.

The payload mounts at rack-local Y=0.09 m, with no added carriage extension. Positive Godot Z is rearward. The horizontal tank is centered at box-local (0, -0.40, 0.38) m, radius 0.3125 m and length 1.426 m, beneath the original rack and behind its hydraulic columns. The sight glass has a separate bottom-anchored `GaugeLiquidLevel` parent: its black fill drains against the ivory backing from full to empty over the accepted burst clock, stays empty while stowing and starts full on a new pickup. The transparent pane, scale and frame remain fixed. This is presentation only; Oil has no capacity resource. The front-facing barrel retains a worn crimson/ivory harlequin wrap around a scalloped ivory painted field. Original black **Laughing Gas** lettering uses short serifs, a warm ochre outline and swept flourishes to form a circus wordmark; red strokes cross out **Gas**, with larger red handwritten **oil** below it. A small rubbed ivory area keeps that correction distinct from the red diamonds at the rear chase angle. Double shoulder pinstripes and small stencil stars reinforce the industrial circus treatment. All artwork is baked into the same 1024×512 Base Color texture on the curved tank: no label backing, brass sign border, raised lettering mesh, additional material or runtime geometry is added. Two passive ribbed hoses join stepped brass adapters with hex nuts, collars and dark gaskets. Worn rubber/rib vertex colors use Godot's Base Color path. The head hose updates a fixed 96-ring/eight-sided mesh with four samples per corrugation, 40 mm rib crests and 27 mm roots without applying forces or constraints. It folds inside the box with the head.

The coil begins at box-local Y=0.105 m and its 1.145 m authored span ends at the extended collar. The head compresses to 48% scale at Y=0.29 m during retraction. The lid opens to 2.35 radians for loose-spring clearance. The mouth opens over five accepted ticks, stays open during the one-second burst, and closes over twelve ticks before head retraction, lid closure and original rack descent. Runtime generates no model collision. `OilJack` uses the cached packed scene through `MatchResourceLoader`; `CarRackPresentation` retains shared mechanism ownership. Core's firing socket is vehicle-local (0, 2.85, 1.845) plus accepted displacement and tilted open-mouth offset (0, -0.055, 0.265). Neither cosmetic tilt nor recoil turns authoritative aim. Core adjusts ballistic elevation to clear the unchanged box, rack and rear body at every permitted spring offset; it retains the moving mouth origin. The short visible neck uses that same clearance rule in the displayed chassis frame before joining accepted world flight beyond the hardware. Near the vehicle, the liquid width is constrained independently of the free-stream width control.

Black spill meshes, flight and splashes are original Godot geometry. `OilFluid.gdshader` is shared through the match loader; each hazard owns its materials. Geometry and contact share `OilSegment.EdgeRadius`. Gameplay ends at the captured deadline, followed by a two-second visual-only dissipation with at most 32 retiring owners.
