# Production rack-mounted Boost jet

Original Trackstorm geometry and materials, authored in Blender 5.2.2. `source/BoostJet.blend` is the editable master. It links `../source/TrackstormCar.blend` as the actual fit reference; the Car is not copied into the export or modified. `source/BuildBoostJet.py` reproducibly constructs the separate assembly, material batches, UVs, pivots, flame shell and preview deployment keys, then exports only the Boost collection to `BoostJet.glb`. Run it with Blender's background `--python` option from the repository. `sources.json` records tool version, source/export hashes and reference hash. The source directory is excluded from Godot import.

Blender uses metres, +Y forward and +Z up. glTF becomes Godot -Z forward/+Y up. The export root is local to WeaponRack; the editable master places that root at the production rack stowed origin for fit. Mount is rack-local Godot (0, -.46, -.12), suspended below the platform by four bolted braces and diagonal stays. The rounded red intake above the rack faces Godot -Z (vehicle forward), with a closed rear panel, recessed grille and swept duct into the compressor. Fuel manifolds, clamps, heat-shield ribs, concentric casing bands, outlet stators and a central bearing ring follow the user-approved [concept](reference/ApprovedBoosterConcept.png). That built-in imagegen design reference is retained unmodified and checksummed in the manifest; its pixels are not used in runtime materials. Outer collar radius is .412 m. The trunk opens before the full-sized hardware is revealed once the underslung chamber clears the bay floor; the barrel extends only after the rack is raised, and nests before the rack lowers.

Keep `Mount/Sleeve`, `Mount/Nozzle`, `Mount/Ram_L`, `Mount/Ram_R`, `Nozzle/Outlet`, `Nozzle/Turbine`, `Nozzle_Boost_Throat` and `Outlet/FlameSurface` names stable: runtime binds these authored nodes. Sleeve moves +.14 m Godot Z, nozzle +.28 m; rods scale Z from 1 to 1+.28/.26. The nested barrel remains overlapping throughout this restrained travel. Outlet is (0,-.46,.395) nested and (0,-.46,.675) extended in rack-local coordinates. Preview keys are retained in Blender, but animations are not exported; runtime owns reversible state-driven motion.

Hardware has smart-projected UVs and original metallic/roughness materials. The 36-by-24 flame shell has explicit circumferential/longitudinal FlowUVs, a lobed profile and 3.4 m authored length. Godot's imported V runs from 1 at the outlet to 0 at the tip; the flame shader accounts for this. Runtime duplicates this shared mesh for the core and wisps and overrides the throat material. Smoke/sparks reuse the existing licensed Kenney textures; see the root third-party registry and [runtime contract](../../../docs/features/boost-exhaust.md).

The 16 swept turbine blades and conical hub are batched beneath the separate Turbine pivot. Runtime rotates this pivot about Godot local Z, idles at 2 radians/s when visible and approaches 10 radians/s during accepted use. Outlet stators remain stationary. The enlarged metal outlet leaves the original flame shell geometry and UVs unchanged.
