## Sustained Machine Gun resource

Every newly acquired Machine Gun starts with the configured discrete ammunition capacity (800 rounds by default) in its physical held slot. Holding **UseItem** after an accepted capability-bound use sustains fire; releasing, switching slots, losing input ownership or disconnecting stops further shots while preserving the exact remaining rounds and fractional firing phase. At the default 80 rounds/second (4,800 RPM), 800 rounds deplete in approximately 10 seconds of actual firing. Exhaustion clears only that physical slot through the ordinary item lifecycle.

The host consumes the current ready [accepted weapon aim](weapon-aiming.md#machine-gun-firing-consumer), adds deterministic spread and queries the Client host's closest native hit over the full pivot-to-range segment. Missing, expired, deploying or self-blocked aim pauses ammunition consumption without catch-up fire. Static geometry and owner-envelope intersections stop the ray; valid living rival hits stage damage and impulse through the existing authority transaction. Defaults remain 225 m range, falloff beginning at 12 m, 2.25 HP per round and 8 N·s knockback. The fixed 0.42-degree cone half-angle targets approximately 80% frame inclusion at 200 m; frame inclusion is distinct from native collision and damage. There is no homing, forced hit quota or visual resampling. Exponent-1.5 falloff reaches zero at 225 m. Perfect close-range hits total 1800 HP per default magazine against a 1000 HP vehicle. Applied rival HP loss uses the shared Circus scoring authority.

Confirmed impacts retain six short-lived orange sparks at the accepted hit point, independently of tracer frequency. The existing burst cap bounds catch-up work; particles fade in 0.22 seconds and their nodes retire at 0.3 seconds. They scatter back along the incoming ray because publication does not carry a surface normal. Impact presentation is independent of the travelling streak pool.

The HUD shows authoritative remaining magazine percentage and never predicts ammunition. Remaining rounds, acquisition capacity and fractional firing phase serialize in the existing item protocol and survive fresh admission, reconnect/resume and host migration without replaying historical shots. Developer Options expose capacity, fire rate, range, damage, falloff start/exponent, spread, knockback and tracer frequency.

## Bullet and tracer presentation

`MachineGunFirePresentation` owns one arena-local MultiMesh with 512 preallocated instances and a 64-flight cap per shooter. It creates no per-round nodes, meshes or materials. Saturation drops new cosmetic flights without queueing a replay burst. Ordinary confirmed rounds draw short, dim 1.1 m streaks; existing authority-selected tracers draw stronger 4.2 m streaks with a narrow, distance-capped width. The default selects every second round, including the first. The original shader tapers both ends and softens the width without adding lights or oversized bullet spheres.

Flights move at a cosmetic 600 m/s from the actual rendered individual barrel tip toward the unchanged confirmed endpoint. They are clipped at that endpoint and expire after at most 550 ms, including a displaced local presentation pose. Damage and impact timing remain immediate authoritative outcomes; these are not new gameplay projectiles. No extra spread, target selection, native ray or ammunition consumption enters presentation. A zero-length accepted segment means cover before the muzzle plane and produces no outgoing streak. A segment whose endpoint is behind its cosmetic origin is also suppressed.

`MachineGunFiringSequence` reconstructs left/right parity from confirmed capacity and remaining rounds, preserving the existing alternating firing order for flashes, recoil, feeds and flights. Batched shots, either inventory slot, release/reuse and reconstructed partial magazines share this rule. The prior confirmed sequence supplies final rounds when exhaustion clears the ammo record. Each flight remains in world space after emission, so moving the car does not drag an old shot along. Release, readiness loss and slot changes produce no new flights; existing flights drain within the lifetime bound. Recovery clears the pool and repeated publications cannot duplicate flights.

The barrel tips are obtained from the production rig including yaw, pitch and recoil. The paired barrels bracket the shared authoritative axis at X ±0.28896 m. Cosmetic convergence to the confirmed endpoint preserves actual spread outcomes while the complete authoritative pivot-to-range collision segment still protects nearby cover. The fixed vehicle rack and Core firing rules retain their existing owners.

`check-machine-gun.ps1 -GodotPath <exe>` is the feature-specific native harness for sustained host/remote firing and short-range native rays. Core tests cover cadence, depletion/reuse, falloff/attribution, invalid ray observations, tuning, two-slot state and checkpoint continuation. These are local-machine checks, not authenticated EOS/WAN/multi-device or long-session balance evidence.

`check-machine-gun-vfx.ps1 -GodotPath <exe> -Motion` renders production barrels with scripted outcomes at 30/60 FPS, checks cardinal/overhead origins and endpoint clipping, and exercises eight simultaneous shooters at default and maximum configured fire rates. It records pool bounds and CPU update timing separately from GPU or network performance. `check-weapon-aim.ps1 -MachineGun -Visual -Impaired` exercises the existing real three-peer Machine Gun aiming scenarios, ordered endpoints, pool delivery on every peer, simultaneous shooters and lifecycle stops. The full aiming harness still runs these scenarios before its wider camera checks.
