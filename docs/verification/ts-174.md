## Assumptions, limitations and unverified areas

- The photo specifies the wall/fence concept, not exact dimensions, paint color or structural engineering. The baked heights/profile are implementation choices, verified with production vehicle physics.
- Detection uses the authoritative vehicle center at fixed ticks and a 1 mm edge tolerance. It is not a swept trigger for arbitrary out-and-back teleports between ticks. Exposure latches until death/reset, interpreting Jira's continuous-until-death requirement. The additional below-map floor catches internal map falls too.
- Fence collision is a continuous sheet at the visible mesh envelope; individual wire openings are not simulated, and the fence is rigid, not deformable. No invisible collider extends above/outside that envelope. Existing exterior scenery is retained but no exterior gameplay was added.
- Developer zero damage intentionally leaves the OOB warning visible, including its hazard wording, while HP stays unchanged. Existing repair and host tuning remain available; those overrides can extend survival.
- Automated native driving and initialized-velocity launches are VERIFIED. Human keyboard/gamepad driving feel, a player-earned weapon-launch trajectory, audio listening, long-session performance profiling and subjective multiplayer latency are UNVERIFIED.
- Separate computers, Internet conditions, authenticated EOS P2P, exported builds and native reconnect/host migration while actively OOB were not run. Checkpoint serialization/continuation and host-only state ownership were tested deterministically; they are not claimed as live migration evidence.
- Ten pre-existing untracked rock texture files were left untouched and excluded from delivery.
