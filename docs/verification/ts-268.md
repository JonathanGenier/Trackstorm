## Implemented

- Keyboard steering retains progressive rise, with faster return (1.5/s) and reversal (3/s). Ground wheel counter-response is separately tunable (2.8 rad/s default).
- Ground steering uses the existing conditioned analog axis, deadzone and response curve. A bounded ground-only sensitivity hook defaults to 1; aerial shaping bypasses it.
- Ground wheel range fades smoothly from 0.9 rad at 8 m/s to 0.225 rad at 35 m/s, using contact-plane speed. Filtering remains rate bounded. Limits, fade speeds and counter-response are Developer Options with validation, persistence and host configuration serialization.
- Ordinary throttle/steering no longer creates PowerSlip or automatic dirt power-slip targets. Legacy snapshot slip decays; physical tire saturation and deliberate handbrake behavior remain. Retired automatic-slip options are removed from the live catalog. Configuration wire version is TC36, 201 options, 1629 bytes.
- Held brake enters reverse once the available braking step can cross the stopping threshold, including gravity-induced downhill creep. Released pedal tails cannot initiate reverse. No grip profiles, aerial controls, player settings UI, or unrelated vehicle baseline were redesigned.

- A 100 ms keyboard correction at 20 m/s produced 0.276 degrees of wheel movement. Held steering accumulated rather than snapping to lock.
- At 40 m/s, native analog raw 0.4 produced 0.0031 rad wheel angle and 0.0503 rad/s yaw; raw 0.575 produced 0.0199 rad and 0.319 rad/s. Small corrections remained distinct.

- Deliberate Dirt handbrake entry reached -2.542 rad/s yaw, then countersteer recovered; neutral steering with throttle settled yaw to 0.0002 rad/s. Handbrake behavior remains deliberate and materially stronger than small ordinary corrections.
