## Approved Round 2 fireball and shockwave revision

The user rejected the compact Round 1 effect and approved sparks leading a much larger, more realistic fireball in the visual style of the existing Boost flame, followed by smoke rising and dissipating. After the contact-only knockback contract was clarified, the user explicitly chose a **larger cinematic wave, clearly cosmetic**. This overrides the earlier contact-sized visual direction; damage, knockback targeting/magnitude, attraction, proximity, collision and physics remain unchanged. Jira TS-250 was updated with the approval and clarification.

### Changed result

- Replaced the fire sprite cluster with two layered spheres using a project-authored procedural noise shader derived from the Boost flame approach. They expand into a roughly 2.85 m radius billowing fireball after the initial spark/flash, then fade into the smoke phase. The shader uses turbulent silhouette and alpha variation; the fireball does not own gameplay state.
- A narrow translucent shockwave expands to roughly 6.25 m and fades in 0.58 seconds. It is intentionally a cinematic visual pulse; the Mine still applies knockback only to the contacted vehicle. No radial damage or impulse was introduced.
- A one-shot smoke emitter starts after ignition, rises and thins via a lifetime alpha ramp. The effect root and all emitters are removed after 2.7 seconds. The existing sixteen-instance presentation cap remains.
- The match resource loader preloads the new shader. The native Mine fixture now records the smoke ascent, dissipation and cleared state in addition to the normal/overlap fireball.

## User-authorized higher, slower smoke correction

The user asked for the smoke to lift higher and dissipate a little more slowly. Only Client presentation and its capture fixture changed: the cap now travels 3.8 m above its starting height over 2.25 seconds (formerly 2.85 m over 1.6 seconds), its opacity fades between 2.05 and 3.65 seconds (formerly 1.55–2.62), and the narrower column stretches upward beneath it. Smoke particle lifetimes increase from 2.45 to 3.25 seconds without increasing particle counts. The effect node frees after 4.2 seconds; the fixture now waits 240 cleanup ticks. Gameplay authority, damage, knockback, attraction, proximity/contact, collision, physics, shockwave and fireball remain unchanged.
