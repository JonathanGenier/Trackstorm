# TS-250 — Proxy Mine explosion VFX overhaul

Verified on `ts-250-jg` after synchronizing with `origin/main` on 2026-09-29. Jira TS-250 had no required children or comments. TS-249's production Mine and placement arm were already present. The later `main` integration also brought TS-227's Mine support-convergence correction; TS-250 changed no authoritative gameplay values or physics.

## Implemented

- Client creates `ProxyMineExplosion` only from the committed Proxy Mine impact in an accepted item publication, at the authoritative Mine position. Other item effects retain their existing path.
- A brief emissive flash and light lead a short contact-sized impulse ring, fire, sparks, small dark fragments and translucent smoke. The ring is presentation of the contacted vehicle's impulse, not a damage-area indicator. No new collision or gameplay state is attached.
- Each explosion frees itself after 1.55 seconds; the presentation prunes expired references and caps concurrent explosion instances at the authoritative sixteen-Mine bound, replacing the oldest cosmetic instance if the cap is reached.
- Extended the native three-peer Mine harness with close detonation captures and two close, distinct authoritative Mine impacts against one vehicle. It checks concurrent effect count, unique impact identities, peer removal and eventual cleanup.
- Updated the durable [held-item feature document](../features/items.md) and Story version to `0.2.10` (`0.2.10.0` export preset) from the synchronized main.

## Assumptions and limitations

- The Mine remains a contact-damage hazard; the ring is a short local impulse cue and does not represent either damage falloff or attraction distance. Existing audio is reused and was not changed.
- Rendered observation used the project's native verification platforms, production chase camera for the multi-Mine trial and a fixture close camera for the impact/overlap images, on an RTX 4070 Ti with Godot's OpenGL Compatibility renderer.
- The overlap fixture directly repositions two existing authoritative Mine states to make close impacts repeatable; ordinary placement and use were exercised earlier in the same run.

## Unresolved risks and explicitly unverified

- No material in-scope defect remained in the exercised scenarios. Quantitative frame times, VRAM and a sustained stress/soak on lower-end hardware remain **UNVERIFIED**.
- Production oval free driving, varied lighting/weather, independent-device WAN/EOS multiplayer, exported builds, other renderers/GPUs and continuous player audio listening remain **UNVERIFIED**. Local native UDP and sampled rendered frames do not establish those experiences.
