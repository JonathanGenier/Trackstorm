- Car 4 spawns 6.096 m (20 feet) behind the existing crate, facing its original
  approach point. It drives forward using existing steering/throttle, acquires
  through ordinary pickup authority, reverses with the ordinary brake/reverse
  input, then parks at its rearward starting position with the item.
- The original three-metre pickup sphere can award an item before the car reaches
  the crate center. The normal single-item trip finishes its approach before
  reversing, so the physical forward/reverse journey remains about 20 feet.
- If the crate is unavailable, Car 4 stops and waits at the approach point until
  collection succeeds. Full inventory returns it home rather than making another
  approach. A parked car stays parked even if its items are used/discarded.
- Destruction and normal respawn restart the trip. Home respawn waits for vehicle
  clearance; initial placement includes 0.3 m suspension clearance on the slightly
  uphill dirt, then native support determines settled height/orientation.
- Only stopped waiting/inspection poses are anchored against drift and impulses.
  Health, targeting, damage, crate breaking, inventory and respawn rules remain
  existing authorities. There is no auto-fire, auto-discard or replacement spawn.
- The existing host recovery supplement now carries Car 4's vehicle/life, phase
  and acquired-item latch. Moving/waiting/returning/parked continuation survives
  serialization; recovery does not restart a completed trip after discard.
- Updated the focused hosted fixture, Core tests and related feature docs. Versions,
  workflow configuration, camera/weapon behavior and unrelated gameplay were not changed.
