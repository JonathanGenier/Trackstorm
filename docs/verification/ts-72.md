## Delivered scale and authoring

The complete existing Trackstorm design was measured at 3.505 m, exported intact for migration, uniformly rescaled by 1.3723252515 in Blender 5.2.2, and imported as a GLB. Editable production Blender source, original migration GLB, conversion script, measurements and provenance are under `assets/vehicles`. Godot's former mesh-authoring script now only generates the material-binding wrapper. Existing CC0 source assets/materials remain in use.

Godot physics nodes remain at unit scale. Both offline and network adapters use the same box envelope from the visible underbody through the roof, with the complete armor/bumper footprint. Wheel rays and Core spring lever arms align with measured tire centers. Center of mass and load-transfer height were converted. Spring/damper rates, vehicle mass, propulsion, braking, speed caps, friction and steering tuning were not retuned. The existing static-wheel/sprung-box approximation remains; wheel articulation is not added.

Host-local Developer Options schema 2 migrates old saved default dimensions (wheelbase, load height, suspension length) once. It preserves custom values and unrelated tuning. Deliberately customized spatial values can still depart from the canonical visual geometry.

## Systems reviewed without behavioral changes

- Missile launch remains at the authoritative vehicle center and excludes its owner; it cannot skip a nearby obstruction through an enlarged muzzle offset.
- Held items at +2.2 m, remote tags at +2.4 m, identification materials and vehicle-centered destruction/effect origins still clear or attach correctly to the body.
- Damage, health, inventory, lifecycle timing and item authority are unchanged.
- Networking continues to replicate metre positions and normalized orientations; no root scale is serialized or applied. Prediction and authority share spatial configuration and collision creation.
- Oval meshes, collision, 6 x 3 m grid rectangles, horizontal spawn coordinates and headings are unchanged. ActiveMap applies the common vehicle clearance lift to all initial/admission/reset/respawn consumers.
- TS-73 handling/asphalt tuning and later map content were not implemented.

## Manual observation and limits

**VERIFIED visual review:** inspected the rendered side, chase and banked-driving images directly. The vehicle retains its design, its tires meet level ground, body clearance is visible and framing remains usable relative to the oval/grid.

**UNVERIFIED human-driven lap:** native Windows capture failed with `SetIsBorderRequired: No such interface supported (0x80004002)`. The completed lap uses the existing automatic steering fixture and is not claimed as a manually keyboard-driven lap or subjective handling acceptance.

Separate-PC/EOS/NAT behavior and a long multiplayer soak were not run. Eight separate local impaired processes previously exceeded the existing p99 correction gate in TS-71 on both the oval and old-map control; that baseline limitation is documented in [TS-71 evidence](ts-71-map-swap.md). No network threshold is relaxed here. Static wheels and the simplified box collider remain intentional existing architecture; driving balance belongs to TS-73.

## Eight-process stress result

The isolated final run with 30 ms outbound delay, 5 ms jitter and 2% loss failed the existing correction-p99 <3 m acceptance gate. Client p99 ranged from 2.966 to 4.736 m; every peer finished grounded. [Stress metrics](ts-72/network-stress.json). No gate was relaxed. This is a measured limitation, not counted as a pass or attributed solely to hardware. The earlier TS-71 old-map control also failed this gate; investigating large-instance networking performance remains separate from vehicle scale conversion.
