## Changes

- Handling lasts **two seconds total** per affected tire by default: 1.65 seconds at full impairment, followed by 0.35 seconds of smooth recovery. Recovery occurs inside the configured total duration and cannot extend it. Front lateral, rear longitudinal and rear lateral grip loss each default to 95%, retaining physical tire forces and player input. Existing Oil developer controls expose total duration, recovery and strength; retuning preserves each wheel's phase.
- Individual affected rubber meshes gain a fading black, glossy coating. Accepted per-wheel timers seed six seconds of cosmetic contamination; continuous overlap and duplicate snapshots do not refresh it. Live duration retuning does not masquerade as a new cosmetic contact.
- Four fixed 32-particle oil emitters per car provide contact splashes and speed/slip/coverage-scaled droplets with 0.55-second lifetime. They have no collision or gameplay callbacks.
- Independent tire paths produce dark, wet strips in the existing shared fixed-capacity mark ring. Native wheel support, surface normals and midpoint support probes gate emission. Missing support, airborne wheels, water, teleports and life boundaries break continuity. The existing eight-metre continuity limit accommodates high-speed sampling. Marks last twelve seconds and fade over the final eight; diminishing contamination also lowers new-mark opacity. Cosmetic residue neither creates hazards nor scores.
- Added the native two-peer tire fixture and fast-check routing; updated Oil timing/default tests, existing handling/options fixtures and affected feature documentation.

#### Recommendation

**Problem:** Oil trail presentation is too uniformly stamped and ribbon-like to read as convincingly wet residue at the inspected distances.

**Evidence:** Both curved-trail images show conspicuous repeated rectangular tread sections. The wet-corner and active countersteer screenshots show long, straight-sided, regularly patterned strips with abrupt endpoints; the black droplets are visible, but the strips dominate the image. This is a visible polish judgment, not a claim of broken contact geometry.

**Severity:** Medium.

**Impact:** The trail communicates where a tire traveled clearly, but its visual character is closer to a strongly printed ordinary tire mark than distinctive oily residue. That weakens the payoff of this Story's main presentation feature.

**Suggested Improvement:** Refine the existing oil-mark appearance with softer lateral/end alpha, less conspicuous segment repetition and restrained coverage variation. Preserve the independent tire path, current lifetime and bounded shared ring. Compare the result with ordinary skid marks at the production chase-camera distance on asphalt and dirt before accepting it; avoid increasing density merely to make the effect louder.
