## Assumptions, limitations and explicitly unverified areas

- Surface ordering applies to canonical defaults. Deliberate host overrides may change ordering or disable grip/drive. Existing saved Concrete/Mud values are preserved; Reset to Defaults + Apply adopts new defaults.
- The slope checks sample usable grades; the 0.55 support-normal cutoff is not a promise that every material climbs every geometrically supported slope. Tires still obey their traction budget.
- Material selection follows the existing center-ray / deterministic wheel fallback rule; no per-wheel material blending was added. Mixed contacts can switch the force profile; they do not reset velocity or steering/handbrake memory.
- Rock was not assigned tuning by this Story and retains neutral baseline. Water depth/drowning/damage/resistance is not implemented or accepted here.
- Native gameplay was actively exercised with synthetic logical input and rendered output. Desktop computer-use capture was attempted twice and failed with `SetIsBorderRequired failed: No such interface supported (0x80004002)`. No successful desktop keyboard/gamepad playtest, human ergonomic/fun judgment or listening test is claimed.
- Authenticated EOS, Internet/multi-device racing, exports, prolonged human sessions and all six materials in separate impaired-network processes are unverified. Each material was exercised through both native adapters; the ordinary separate-process route covers its existing oval/infield drive.
- No production dependencies, assets, map geometry, network authority owner or prediction architecture were replaced.
