from the exported driving surfaces; grid paint has no collision. The eight
stable markers follow existing arena conventions and face +X. The original
master grid was relocated to the flat straight, with no geometry inferred from
concept art.

## Limitations and interpretation

- The retained local master matches the Jira attachment's exact name and
  1,178,873-byte size. Its SHA-256 is recorded in the source manifest. Direct
  remote download returned HTTP 403, so remote/local **byte equality is
  UNVERIFIED**. The matching identity is inferred from that metadata and the
  supplied geometry, whose dimensions/banking also match the Story.
- The Blender build emitted a Windows thumbnail-cache write error while saving;
  the actual production blend saved and was subsequently reopened and verified.
  This affects the external thumbnail cache, not the delivered mesh or exports.
- One automated native lap establishes foundation drivability, not subjective
  human handling quality, high-speed racing balance or multiplayer acceptance
  on this map. These were not substituted for the later vehicle/map Stories.
- The reusable foundation is not wired into live matchmaking or practice.
  Existing scenes remain intact. Edges are intentionally open; no final barriers,
  infield gameplay, terrain dressing, textures or lighting polish were added.
- No functional scope was deferred. The plan/surface and footprint distinctions
  above are explicit measurement interpretations, not silent source changes.

See [authoring workflow](../../assets/maps/oval/README.md) for the complete
Blender → GLB → Godot import → collision/scene bake procedure, including the
warning that rebuilding from the original master overwrites later manual edits.
