## User-approved refinement — 2026-10-03 (Round 2)

The user accepted the initial result and authorized raising the chase-distance minimum to 1.15 while preserving the maximum, plus making pullback more progressive so small jumps do not cause a conspicuous short zoom-out. This section supersedes the initial minimum/default and aerial-distance response above. Jira TS-279 was updated with the approved refinement; all other scope remains unchanged.

Implementation `83fe0cad`:

- Chase distance now defaults to and clamps at **1.15**, maximum **1.5**. Missing, nonfinite and older below-minimum values resolve to 1.15 through the existing settings validation. This corresponds to a 7.36–9.6 m authored horizontal boom.
- Rotation stabilization remains early (0.12 seconds), independently of distance. Extra distance stays zero for 0.35 seconds, then the target follows a smoothstep ramp over one additional second of airtime. Outward convergence is 4/s. Sustained jumps still reach the original 3.6 m default / 5.4 m maximum extra distance.
- Landing stops further expansion immediately and returns at 6/s. A backward landing holds only the distance already earned while the heading catches up; a short hop cannot request full pullback during recovery.
- Added repeated half-second-hop coverage at 30/60/144 Hz, including maximum strength, no continued expansion after contact, and subsequent full-distance flight. Extended the native two-car fixture to 16 phases with isolated and repeated small jumps. Camera/obstruction fixture baselines now reflect the approved 1.15 default.
