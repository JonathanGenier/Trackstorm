## Assumptions and limitations

- Oil is a bounded ballistic/surface-contact approximation, not a fluid-volume solver. Small supported planar pieces model the deposited coverage; support probing can shrink or discard impacts at discontinuities. Cosmetic impact droplets do not create independent hazards.
- Native launch/turning cases include fixture-authored poses; ordinary native driving, gravity and collision are exercised where identified. These checks do not establish physical keyboard/controller ergonomics or competitive balance.
- Ready-phase cosmetic spring/deployment progress and native solver contact caches are not serialized. A resumed active pour reconstructs its exposed mechanism and retained authoritative flight instead of replaying pickup/use.
- Complete item updates retain reliable-channel latency limitations. No WAN, independent-device or authenticated EOS claim is made.
