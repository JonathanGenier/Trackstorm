### Limitations

Oil remains a bounded parcel/planar-footprint approximation. Support probes can shrink or discard hits at ledges and nonplanar discontinuities. A native ramp sweep checks supported impact position/normal; a driven ramp crest is not verified. Footprint rendering samples the analytic organic edge with 64 perimeter points. Cosmetic sheets/splashes do not add hazards. Local UDP evidence does not establish authenticated EOS, WAN, multi-device operation or long-session soak. Runtime capture readback affects frame time; repeated-use samples are not a hardware benchmark. - Complete item updates retain reliable-channel latency limitations. No WAN, independent-device or authenticated EOS claim is made.
