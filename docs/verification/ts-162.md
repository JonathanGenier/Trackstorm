## Limitations and unresolved observations

**Migration admission risk:** repeated three-peer runs sometimes timed out at
stage 61 with `authority fencing could not be established`, before the environment
recovery assertions. Other runs completed exact restoration. Preparation stalls
relative to the existing two-second host-silence policy are a possible factor,
but the root cause is **unverified**. The successful run is not evidence that this
intermittent failure has been fixed. No timeout/authority policy redesign was made.

The broad `-Case Tabletop` run failed `TabletopSide-1_-1` on a damaging contact
near (-38.34, 1.21, -33.33), HP 986.90. That route passes an intact authored rock
near (-41.36, -0.10, -33.81), outside the edited lip region. This run is **not**
reported as passing. Direct lip/deck/jump cases passed. No baseline execution
proved the broad failure predates this Story; integrated route tuning remains a
TS-163 consideration. No unrelated rock relocation or vehicle tuning was made.

Controlled horizontal motion trades free-body realism for safe bounded response.
Mesh swaps are abrupt, with no added fracture VFX or dedicated rock-break audio.
There is one smaller reusable piece, not a fragment cloud. Matching-build scene
ordering supplies stable identities.

## Round 2 — approved human-test refinement

Jira TS-162 was re-read in full before changes (updated 2026-09-25 12:34).
The approved refinement requires stronger severity-scaled vehicle damage,
size-dependent progressive degradation and believable bounded splitting. The
user reported human confirmation that weapons, plants, final movable rocks and
the foundation work well; this is user-provided evidence, not an agent test.
No new Story, branch, dependencies, assets or damage authority were introduced.
The existing tabletop terrain correction is unchanged.

### Implemented refinement

- Accepted horizontal closing severity now yields
  `min(360, 10 * max(0, severity - 3)^1.5)` damage. Normalizing the horizontal
  projection of sloped faces preserves solid impacts; tangential brushes and
  near-horizontal support contacts remain harmless. Low stones under the chassis
  use the existing oriented footprint, with actual tagged contacts taking
  precedence. Vehicle contact cooldown remains twelve ticks. Committed weapon
  impacts retain their existing explosion rules and are not suppressed by that
  vehicle cooldown.
- Every root reserves four deterministic piece slots. The first breaks produce
  two appropriately scaled BoulderLow pieces; subsequent breaks can populate the
  remaining slots. At the four-piece cap, further damage reduces existing pieces.
  Each piece receives damage independently. Stage size multiplies by 0.72;
  authored starting diameter and the minimum determine depth. Production minimum
  diameter is 0.3271603 m; its largest profile reaches stage 11. Near-minimum roots
  start as one terminal movable piece, without manufacturing extra tiny fragments.
- Intact health stays 180, stage-two health stays 60, and later health falls to a
  floor of 20. Excess damage never skips a stage. Damage is collected before
  transitions, so newborn pieces cannot cascade within the same accepted batch.
- Production reserves 588 slots for 147 roots. At most sixteen pieces move, at
  most 6 m/s and within eight metres of the original root. The existing shallow
  wheel-only support and chassis exclusion remain. No free rigid-body debris,
  solver piles or runtime fracturing were added. Vegetation behavior is unchanged.
- Sparse version-two TD state encodes dormant slots in one byte, active slots in
  29 bytes. Production bounds are 5,033 bytes initially / 17,381 bytes with every
  slot active; the hard maximum is 30,231 bytes. Existing TR-v3 checkpoints,
  admission, generation/epoch fencing and host migration carry the expanded state.
