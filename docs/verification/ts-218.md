## Assumptions and limitations

The operational wall defaults are 6 x 2.5 x 0.6 m, 250 kg, 1 m rear clearance, exposed in
existing Configs. Live tuning affects future deployments, not installed shapes/mass. Native
friction/damping use a simple non-bouncing metal prop response. This is not final balancing.
Walls can topple and thereby lose their upright cover height; that is intentional free rigid
motion, not a static obstacle guarantee. The full collider activates immediately while the
0.2-second wing expansion is cosmetic. Recovered walls appear expanded without historical use.

Explosions retain the existing non-occluded radial vehicle damage contract; the wall blocks
projectile/bullet paths, not every radial blast behind it. Native contact solver caches are not
serialized, only committed pose and velocity. Complete reliable item motion can pause under
late delivery and has no separate wall prediction/interpolation stream.
