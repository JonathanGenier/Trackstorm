with longest flights 0.87/1.07/1.30 s and peak world-origin height 5.04 m at 18 m/s.

**VERIFIED:** all six retuned jumps finish grounded at 1000 HP and land on dirt
downslope. West 16 m/s: 2.00 s flight, 25.86 m travel, 6.40 m origin clearance.
West 18 m/s: 2.28 s, 33.28 m, 7.42 m. East 18 m/s: 2.28 s, 33.31 m, 7.43 m.

Both jump sets repeated successfully during the full route run.
[West measurements](ts-142/west-jumps.txt), [east measurements](ts-142/east-jumps.txt).

Clearance is origin-to-terrain, not tire or underside clearance. Normal level
ride height is 0.9 m. Launch position is sampled on the fourth unsupported tick;
reported horizontal travel excludes the first three unsupported ticks. World
origin peaks (~10.56 m at 18 m/s) are not height above local terrain.

**VERIFIED:** all ten main routes, 2,598 lane support probes, overlapping shortcuts,
continuous loop tour and two-car tunnel traversal passed. The southern basin

**VERIFIED:** upright, yawed lateral travel, yaw spin, 35°/45° roll, 25°/35° pitch
and banked landings retain full HP across both adapters and slopes. Body contacts
include ~16.32 m/s bottom-out severity. Side, roof, front/rear and initially

The existing 22,000 N·s off-centre tumble impulse is an extreme synthetic fixture.

## Assumptions and limitations

Native logical-input driving, controlled drop/roll/impulse probes and rendered
inspection are verified. They do not establish human keyboard/controller
ergonomics or every organically induced jump/crash combination. Damage and
handling are deliberately unchanged. No low-end performance claim is made.
Pre-existing `InputButtons.cs` formatting and grass import edits are preserved
and excluded from the Story commit.

## Unresolved risks

The full infield suite still fails the accepted uphill-start limitation. Moving
route traversal is not proof of stop/restart recovery everywhere. The 15 m
recovery zone is traversal space, not guaranteed stopping distance at every speed.
The native network run contains one large startup correction and scheduling
stalls; zero visible snaps or performance regression absence is not claimed.

## Explicitly unverified

Approaches above 18 m/s, all lateral offsets, reverse jump use, dense opposing
combat, every off-route recovery and audio listening remain unverified. Human
control acceptance is recorded below; devices and scenarios were not enumerated.
Both collision adapters were tested locally;
separate-process networking does not exercise every jump/crash remotely.
Internet/EOS, independent devices and exported builds remain unverified.
Lighting/materials, generic chase-camera behavior and environment dressing are
unchanged; the terrain task does not claim final visual dressing.
