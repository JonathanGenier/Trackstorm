# TS-105 verification

## Implemented

- Core now publishes bounded, category-attributed post-multiplier Circus award deltas for Kill, Collision, Drift, Airtime, Long Jump and Top Speed. The version-four match codec validates and round-trips those presentation events without moving scoring authority out of Core.
- Shared Core standings and frozen results carry banked Circus score. Live ranking is score descending, then kills descending, deaths ascending and stable PlayerId; the recorded winner remains first in frozen results.
- The standings board adds a fractional-safe Score column. The gameplay HUD shows the local authoritative banked total and K/D multiplier, category-keyed pending/banked/lost rows, 1.8-second linger and 0.5-second fade. Initial publications do not replay historical awards, and every accepted revision is drained so short-lived deltas are not skipped.
- Match, standings and HUD feature documentation now describes the implemented authority, wire and presentation contracts. Story version is `0.1.21` and the Windows preset representation is `0.1.21.0`.

## Assumptions and limitations

- Trackstorm currently has one network match scoring mode, so an accepted network `MatchState` identifies Circus presentation; local practice has no match boundary and retains the unchanged non-Circus HUD.
- Final results keep the recorded kill-target winner first; score is the primary ordering rule while the match is live and the deterministic secondary key for remaining final rows.
- Native standings uses authoritative score/pending fixtures to isolate replication and rendering. Separate match/stunt harnesses exercise physical scoring sources.
- The main-scene smoke warning occurred during forced timed shutdown; no warning occurred in the dedicated HUD, standings, match, stunt or reconnect harnesses.

## Unresolved risks
