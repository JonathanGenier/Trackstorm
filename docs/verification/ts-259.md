## Implemented

- Edited the retained TS-164 `TrackstormCar.blend`; did not regenerate it with the
  historical builder. Retained wheel carriers/spin pivots, dual shocks, split
  trunk hinges, sockets, separate lamps and identification surface.
- Replaced broad quarter shoulders with narrower curved panels/arch armor;
  96×32 smooth tire carcasses, 48 staggered tread stations and sidewall lugs.
  Added cage rails, guards, fasteners, vents, ventilated exhaust shields,
  crowned bonnet geometry, longer rear glass/guard and trunk stencils.
- Deep-red paint, lower roughness, specular/clearcoat and sparse oxide accents;
  independently rougher steel. Existing rubber textures are reused.
- Raised rack origin from 0.82 m to **1.34 m**, with mounts at **1.51 m**;
  travel is 1.42 m. Added two nested sleeve stages per hydraulic column.
- Independent `vehicle.trunk_deployment_speed` and
  `vehicle.rack_deployment_speed`, both default 3, finite range 0.1–10.
  Default phase durations are **0.24 s trunk / 0.2933 s rack**, versus
  **0.72 / 0.88 s** in TS-164. Closing uses the respective rates, preserves
  reverse order, and live retuning preserves pose. Frame remainder carries
  across the phase boundary.
- Normal catalog/default/reset/persistence/authority/recovery paths carry both
  values; UI edits feed the actual host and observer vehicle mechanisms.
  Configuration codec is version 25, 173 keys/1405 bytes; discovery bucket is
  `trackstorm-lobby-16`. Item authority/firing timing and collision shapes are
  unchanged.
- `ExportCar.py` exports a batched temporary copy without overwriting the editable
  master. Final master audit: 578 mesh objects, 154,344 vertices, 150,200 polygons;
  game export: 117 meshes. These are authoring counts, not GPU performance metrics.

The overall judgment is weighted toward this Story's primary visual objective;
passing tests do not compensate for the remaining reference mismatch.

**What was exercised:** VERIFIED native driving/articulation, clearance samples,
rendered reference-angle comparisons, item presentation across two UDP peers,
actual UI speed edits reaching owner/observer mechanisms, and production-map
observe/drive/observe segments. Mechanical continuity across all possible poses
and networks remains INFERRED beyond the sampled checks.

**High — integrated body/reference fidelity.** Side and high-angle captures show
large open wheel wells and distinct rounded fender pods around a narrow central
body. The references have fuller, more integrated quarter/fastback surfaces and
denser armored construction. This materially weakens recognition even though the
arches are narrower and smoother than TS-164. Suggested improvement: reshape
quarter-to-door and rear sail transitions against the side/orthographic reference,
retain audited wheel travel, and refine the stance/body volume together.
**In Scope; Existing Task corrective work.**

**Medium — surface and mechanical detail.** Bonnet/roof/lid paint is red and glossy,
but bright highlights can read pink and broad smooth surfaces look cleaner and
more uniform than the intended weathered vehicle. Bumper/skid, scoop, exhaust,
wheel faces and panel joins remain simplified. Suggested improvement: add
reference-specific plate layering, perforated scoop/skid construction, localized
edge wear/scratches and less uniform painted-panel response while keeping red
as the dominant color. **In Scope; Existing Task corrective work.**

**Recommended Next Round:** a focused body/quarter/fastback and material-detail
pass using the committed multi-angle captures beside all ten references, then
repeat geometry clearance and runtime observation. Do not redesign driving physics
or add final weapon art. Await explicit human authorization for another round.

### Limitations / unverified areas

- No physical human-controller session, remote multi-PC/EOS session, export-build
  playtest or broad GPU/frame-time benchmark was performed.
- Tire/chassis intersection checks sample surfaces; they are not exhaustive swept
  collision proofs. Unchanged support rays remain inboard of visible wheel centers.
- Reflections were observed in the current Compatibility renderer/daylight setup;
  material appearance in every environment preset is unverified.
- Existing placeholder representations for weapons without final art remain.
- Screenshots show sampled poses; they do not establish every animation frame or
