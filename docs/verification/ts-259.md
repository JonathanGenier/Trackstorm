  historical builder. Retained wheel carriers/spin pivots, dual shocks, split
  trunk hinges, sockets, separate lamps and identification surface.

  96×32 smooth tire carcasses, 48 staggered tread stations and sidewall lugs.
  Added cage rails, guards, fasteners, vents, ventilated exhaust shields,
  crowned bonnet geometry, longer rear glass/guard and trunk stencils.
- Deep-red paint, lower roughness, specular/clearcoat and sparse oxide accents;
  independently rougher steel. Existing rubber textures are reused.
- Raised rack origin from 0.82 m to **1.34 m**, with mounts at **1.51 m**;
  travel is 1.42 m. Added two nested sleeve stages per hydraulic column.
- Independent `vehicle.trunk_deployment_speed` and
  `vehicle.rack_deployment_speed`, both default 3, finite range 0.1–10.
  Default phase durations are **0.24 s trunk / 0.2933 s rack**, versus
  **0.72 / 0.88 s** in TS-164. Closing uses the respective rates, preserves
  reverse order, and live retuning preserves pose. Frame remainder carries
  across the phase boundary.
- Normal catalog/default/reset/persistence/authority/recovery paths carry both
  values; UI edits feed the actual host and observer vehicle mechanisms.
  Configuration codec is version 25, 173 keys/1405 bytes; discovery bucket is
  `trackstorm-lobby-16`. Item authority/firing timing and collision shapes are
  unchanged.
- `ExportCar.py` exports a batched temporary copy without overwriting the editable
  master. Final master audit: 578 mesh objects, 154,344 vertices, 150,200 polygons;
  game export: 117 meshes. These are authoring counts, not GPU performance metrics.

### Changes and measured tradeoffs

- Retained the editable production master and re-exported its material-batched GLB.
  Wheelbase remains **3.101105 m**, with the longer doors/cabin and fastback retained.
- Reduced wheel radial coordinates by **0.54 / 0.582**, a **7.2% diameter reduction**.
  Axial coordinates, **0.526 m rubber width** and centres at **X +/-1.16 m** remain
  unchanged. Runtime rolling radius/vertical placement use 0.54 m.
- Lowered the arch armor crown from approximately **0.52 m** to **0.32 m front /
  0.20 m rear**, relative to the model origin. Opening lips reach **0.22 / 0.15 m**.
  The shoulder is **0.425 m**, down from 0.495 m. Tightened longitudinal openings,
  refitted liners, flattened/tapered shoulders and closed the quarter-to-body ends.
  Approximate settled vertical tire-to-lip gap is **0.29 m front / 0.22 m rear**, down
  from roughly 0.46 m. These are geometry estimates, not a dynamic minimum-distance proof.
- The front lip flares farther outward to retain full steering at compression.
  Total fender envelope is approximately **3.18 m** wide; tire width/track did not
  change. This tradeoff leaves broad shoulders in top/front views and is a visual
  limitation, not a claim that every fender dimension became smaller.
- Identified the penetrating bars as lower control arms and twin shock members.
  Retained these components and rebuilt their unit-axis meshes/coils. Added named
  inboard carrier-local mounts, knuckle brackets and central hub spindle stubs.
  Rear attachments sit 0.39 m inboard of each wheel centre, beyond the rubber's
  0.263 m half-width. Front shock mounts/anchors were further inset for steering.
- Fixed link scaling: rotation now multiplies local-axis length scaling. The old
  chassis-axis scaling displaced endpoints after rotation. Runtime bars now end
  at their moving mounts, including steering, driving and post-landing poses.
- Kept materials, split trunk, rack height/longitudinal placement, independent 3x
  deployment defaults, Dev Options, item presentation and authoritative physics/
  networking intact. No new third-party assets, physics rules or branding.
