### Diagnosis and correction

The initial implementation retained only about 0.052 m of peak vertical displacement on a hard impact, viewed from an approximately 16 m chase radius. A 20 HP hit had an even smaller linear envelope. Those values can be numerically nonzero yet visually negligible. The original tests asserted positive internal shake rather than useful movement in the actual rendered view; the user's playtest exposed that gap.

- Added horizontal movement to the vertical damped impulse, in the camera's view plane. Differing frequencies avoid a repetitive vertical bob; the vector stays inside its envelope.
- Increased the maximum presentation displacement from 0.12 m to a hard-clamped 0.65 m. Actual native events in the focused sequence peak at about 0.28 m. Square-root collision/damage response makes intermediate severities readable; damage gain is 0.85. The 3 m/s contact threshold, envelope coalescing, cooldown, damage-sequence and network contact-tick gates remain intact.
- Camera basis, aim, horizon, follow distance, depth and inertia remain unchanged. No authoritative, physics, input, settings persistence or network message behavior changed.
- Added `check-camera-shake.ps1` and a native verification scene. A second, unshaken reference camera follows the identical displayed vehicle state; checks compare actual camera transforms and projected vehicle position, rather than reconstructing the intended effect from its internal scalar. The check is routed automatically for camera and its own scene/script changes.
- `-Visual` records the real rendered sequence at 60 FPS. The final recording is 1152×648; displacement measurements below are normalized to 720p. The sequence is reproducible synthetic native play, not a claim of free-form human play.

## Assumptions, limitations and risks

- 100% intentionally retains existing response; the setting does not amplify beyond the established maximum. Minor-contact threshold and damage/severity gains are preserved rather than retuned.
- The existing contact and accepted-damage channels coalesce through the strongest envelope; this change adds no new authoritative collision identity or cross-channel event matching.
- Rendered screenshots and synthetic native controls establish operation and layout, not long-session human comfort or physical-controller ergonomics. Existing camera obstruction limitations remain out of scope.
- UNVERIFIED: authenticated EOS, separate physical devices, exported builds, prolonged comfort/accessibility playtesting, and exact predicted-versus-confirmed damaging-collision traces under reconciliation. Local UDP impairment checks do not substitute for those scenarios.
- No new dependencies or assets. Core changes are confined to established local preference data/serialization, with no engine dependency or authoritative simulation change.
