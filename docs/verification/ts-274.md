### Root cause and reproduction boundary

**VERIFIED:** dense terrain collision causes expensive native convex motion sweeps during sustained steering contact with the steep dirt bank. Temporary profiling isolated individual `PhysicsServer3D.BodyTestMotion` calls around 80–90 ms. A hosted-adapter 12 m/s bank approach with held throttle/steering measured mean 16.525 ms / p95 94.855 ms contact observation on the original collider, versus 3.355 / 9.287 ms with the baked collider. The profiling instrumentation was removed. See [original profile](ts-274-evidence/profile.log) and [baked comparison](ts-274-evidence/active-bank-turn.log).

The final two-world impaired UDP fixture was also run with the original collider reconstructed from the unchanged render mesh. Its medium-speed steering trial **failed** the contact-processing gate at mean 16.389 ms / p95 52.592 ms. The baked version passed all three trials, with corresponding medium-speed mean 6.313 ms / p95 18.727 ms. This is a controlled geometry comparison, not a networking redesign. See [original expected failure](ts-274-evidence/world-network-original.log) and [baked result](ts-274-evidence/world-network.log).

**UNVERIFIED:** the precise cause of the human-reported persistent physical trapping/jumping. Original geometry also separated in controlled impact/reverse trials. The slowdown was reproduced; a persistent physical trap was not conclusively reproduced. Reduced sweep cost and the passing recovery tests do not prove every reported trapping condition fixed.

### Collision matrix and native geometry evidence

The 128-case matrix uses the actual production map through both practice and hosted collision adapters: 7 targets × 3 initial speeds (3/12/35 m/s, approximately 11/43/126 km/h) × 3 body orientations (0/0.45/1.57 radians relative to approach), plus held-steering bank trials in both adapters. Targets are the inside tunnel dirt face, bank neck, lateral bank slope, tunnel pillar, retaining wing, outer perimeter and intact placed rock. Pillar/wing/perimeter/rocks provide representative authored static objects.

Each 660-tick case includes sustained throttle, neutral, reverse, another throttle interval and final reverse. Angled solid impacts start half a tick before native swept contact so low-speed lateral friction does not turn a supposed impact into a miss. Reported speeds are initial conditions, not guaranteed actual contact speeds. The intact rock fixtures keep rocks solid; the rendered playtest additionally exercises normal destruction.

Final matrix: maximum native measured penetration 0.036775 m; zero frames above 0.1 m; maximum displacement per tick 0.930554 m. All bank-face/pillar/perimeter/rock cases registered solid contact and reversed more than 2 m from the initial contact region. Among the 107 cases with at least 30 contact frames, largest per-case mean processing cost was 7.981 ms and largest p95 was 19.263 ms. Brief-contact cases can have higher averages; they are not sustained-performance passes. Practice timings exclude native rigid-body solving outside the measured callback.

Two low-speed sideways bank-neck trials had no chassis contacts and are coverage attempts, not verified impacts. High-speed neck/side-slope traversals include intended ramp flight (up to 19.37 m/s upward velocity); an isolated jump is not classified as pathological oscillation. Some final contacts are with the opposite tunnel face after reversing; recovery is measured from the original contact region. The fixture logs motion and normals for diagnosis, and does not prove every possible instability from its bounded penetration/displacement gates alone.

The oval fixture independently casts 14,270 bidirectional centroid normal rays between native original and baked terrain shapes, measuring maximum distance 0.00764 m. Offline auditing samples **all** vertices and face centroids in both directions. Source/render/collision hashes are checked. All 916 rim sections and the existing authored road checks remain covered.

## Limitations, failed checks and unverified areas

- The exact original persistent trap remains unverified. Geometry complexity is the demonstrated cause of sweep stalls, and only an inferred contributor to the human-reported trapped/unstable experience. Human confirmation of the original repro remains valuable before acceptance.
- The destructible-environment suite still hardcodes 147 root rocks / 588 slots / 2,447 plants. The unchanged current map has 157 / 628 / 2,288. Its `actual production rock/plant coverage` assertion is the sole final failure. The historical TS-267 report records the same stale count issue, and neither dressing source nor placement assets changed here. The fixture's later text mentioning old counts is hardcoded and is not current inventory evidence. Unlike that historical run, this run had no managed-handle errors. No full destructible-suite PASS is claimed, and the unrelated count repair was left out of scope.
- The related map-budget native inventory check also failed its existing `multimesh_instances >= 2447 + 864` threshold, as in TS-267. Its assertion occurs before writing the inventory/success result. No complete map-budget pass is claimed; the independent terrain triangle/hash/distance checks did pass. This additional check finished before Astra's final score.
- An initial final-matrix run used stale imported terrain despite an ordinary import scan; a resource-path/count assertion now rejects that condition. After explicit terrain reimport, four low/medium angled rock trials missed their obstacles; correcting their swept starting distance produced the final 128-case pass. An early oval run rejected the old exact render/collision vertex-equivalence assertion; this was replaced with hash-bound bounded geometry and independent native checks appropriate to the collision bake. These initial failures are not presented as passing evidence.
- Baking changes physical surface fit. The 4 cm/2 mm limits are sampled distances, not a mathematical continuous Hausdorff guarantee. Every placement/contact point and custom tuning combination is not exhaustively verified.
- Measured CPU costs depend on this host and scheduling; no universal frame-time guarantee. No long-duration stress soak, exported builds, remote machines, Internet/EOS collision sessions, or physical-controller ergonomics were verified. Existing main-scene startup is a smoke, not a full frontend match playthrough.
- Story acceptance is not claimed complete solely from automated passes while the original physical symptom and the unrelated inventory-suite failures remain unresolved.
