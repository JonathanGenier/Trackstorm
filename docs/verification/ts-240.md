## Astra Runtime Critique - Story Round 1

**Overall Score: 7.5 / 10. Quality Assessment: FAIL (<8.0).** The functional integration is strong, but the observed art finish and stress-frame pacing do not yet justify the repository's highly polished threshold. This is not a claim of production-quality acceptance.

| Category | Score | Runtime basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Runtime functionality / integration | 8.5 | Early presses rejected, no queued shot or refill, full empty stow before all three replacement types, repeated launch and cleanup. |
| Animation / motion | 8.0 | Legible staged extension, symmetrical fins, centered return and practical full-turn articulation; airborne and landing checks retained the mount. |
| Controls / usability | 8.0 | Readiness gate and local/shared tuning semantics behaved predictably in native checks. Human controller feel remains unverified. |
| Multiplayer experience | 8.0 | Two rendered UDP peers agreed on lifecycle; impaired item/terrain checks covered repeated accepted launches. Internet experience is unverified. |
| Visual quality | 6.8 | Circus colors, star fins and mechanical silhouette are readable, but bright uniform metal/ivory surfaces are markedly simpler than the battered reference and production Car. Close flame resolves into soft overlapping orange lobes. |
| Performance / frame pacing | 7.0 | Effects are bounded and removed; 16-effect stress raised median and p95 frame times above the matching baseline. |

**What Was Exercised:** VERIFIED scripted native production Car/arena, compact/deployed/return captures, driving/turning, fixture-authored airborne/native landing, full-turn articulation, two-peer replacement lifecycle, repeated impacts, live tuning/reset and 16 concurrent VFX. INFERRED general fit from measured compact bounds plus sampled rendered poses, not exhaustive collision clearance at every pitch/body attitude. UNVERIFIED human free-play satisfaction, ramp-driven jumping, Internet/EOS, physical multi-PC clients, exported release, low-end GPUs and long-session profiling. Full limitations and the pre-existing mine teardown diagnostics are recorded above.

**Problem 1:** Reference-level material depth and flame finish remain below the desired final-art standard. **Evidence:** final ready and close-flight captures versus the retained extended Missile reference show uniform bright bands/rods and a soft lobed plume. **Severity:** Medium. **Impact:** the weapon reads correctly but looks cleaner and less physically convincing than the Car/reference. **Suggested Improvement:** an authored roughness/weathering and material-value pass in Blender, plus a coherent tapered flame surface with controlled breakup, verified at chase-camera and close range. **Scope:** In Scope. **Corrective Work Type:** Existing Task.

**Problem 2:** Short simultaneous-effect stress misses consistent 60 FPS on this hardware. **Evidence:** matching baseline 16.67/16.67 ms median/p95 versus 20.89/34.58 ms with 16 effects and two rendered arenas. **Severity:** Medium. **Impact:** heavy simultaneous flight can reduce responsiveness and visual smoothness. **Suggested Improvement:** profile the actual single-player viewport and GPU costs, then apply measured mesh/particle distance scaling and material sharing where effective; repeat the same stress and cleanup cases. **Scope:** In Scope. **Corrective Work Type:** Existing Task.

**Recommended Next Round:** If explicitly authorized, prioritize the material/flame finish and measured simultaneous-effect performance, then conduct human driving/firing play before a second critique. No corrective round was started after this formal assessment. Existing forward-launch aiming remains TS-263 scope; explosion/audio remain excluded.
