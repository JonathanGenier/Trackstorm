### Changes

- Blender-authored nested body: `ForwardSleeve` follows the front body, `RearSleeve` follows the engine, and a fixed `MiddleSleeve` provides the central crimson shell and gold seal collars. Closed cylinder surfaces maintain overlap throughout extension. Runtime body/fin travel, readiness, turret/mount transforms, authority and one-shot/stow code were not changed.
- No missile or launcher scale increase. Whole compact bounds exactly match the Round 1 manifest: Godot X -0.306..0.285, Y -0.180..0.355, Z 1.271..2.326 m. The rebuilt launcher retains identical hierarchy/materials/transforms; mesh names changed through Blender regeneration and numerical mesh data differs by at most 5.97e-8 due to exporter rounding.
- Continuous tapered exhaust uses three crossed shader surfaces, gold/yellow hot-core highlights and animated orange/red edges. Twenty supporting flame particles replace the prior 48; the existing flame length/width controls size both layers.
- Default red confetti increased from 32 to 56 live particles and nominal size from 65 to 85 mm. Brighter red and a brief delay before edge charring preserve recognizable paper before rapid burn-away. Lifetime remains 0.38 seconds. Dark crimson smoke and red embers remain. Default total GPU particle capacity is 148 (previously 152), plus three flame quads per missile; no detached effect owner was introduced.
