# TS-148 — Arcing Salvo and owner-local impact marker

**Current evidence: Story Round 2 below supersedes Round 1's six-tick cadence and whole-volley target lock.** The earlier sections remain historical evidence of the original implementation.

## Scope and implementation

Inspected Jira TS-148 and parent TS-115, their descriptions/comments (no required child tasks), applicable agent instructions, workflow/standards/critique, and relevant architecture, item, networking, configuration, HUD and recovery documentation. The Story branch is `ts-148-jg`. Refreshed and merged current `main` at `927520c`, including TS-147 Proxy Mine and swept pickup acquisition. Story version is `0.1.56`.

- Registered Salvo as identity 6 through the existing inventory, category allocation, HUD and tuning paths. Proxy Mine retains identity 5. Added an original SVG with provenance; existing rocket, particle and audio assets are reused.
- Default use atomically reserves five unique rounds. Launches are three metres above the current vehicle, six ticks apart, toward the host-selected point 65 metres forward at the original firing boundary. No manual target-selection packet exists.
- The parabola rises twelve metres above its chord, with approximate mean speed 85 m/s. Host segment sweeps resolve impacts. Each blast has seven-metre radius, 65 maximum HP damage, distance falloff and at most 3,500 N s impulse.
- Only the local owner gets the terrain-sampled circular guide. It previews selected Salvo and retains committed flight targets until their rounds end. Marker identity, geometry and visibility are never serialized.
- Runtime controls cover count, cadence, range, launch height, arc, speed, blast radius, damage/falloff, impulse and marker scale/width/lift. The combined catalog has 131 keys; configuration protocol is 16 and item protocol is 9. Exact game-version admission remains required.
- Existing authoritative health transactions produce distinct attributed damage outcomes for each target/round. Existing lethal-hit attribution recognizes Salvo. No TS-148-specific Circus damage scoring was added; TS-155 owns that shared path.
- Existing checkpoints retain scheduled and flying rounds, target, origin, progress, owner life and IDs. Reset/death/departure and match completion clear applicable rounds. Straight Missile keeps its existing motion and damage settings.

## Assumptions, risks and delivery

- The marker denotes the intended fixed target; intervening obstacles may cause earlier impact. Missing target terrain or the shared projectile cap retains the item. Unsupported marker segments are omitted rather than drawn across empty space.
- Count/target/cadence/arc are captured at use; pending launches read current launch height/speed, while impact/marker properties use current tuning. These semantics are documented in the feature guide.
- Shared item-damage Circus scoring remains TS-155 scope; attributed damage outcomes are available to that shared path.
- No new external dependency or asset was acquired. Core remains engine-independent with Client-to-Core dependency direction and no Shared layer.
- The pre-existing `assets/maps/oval/grass_variation.png.import` edit belongs to the user and is excluded from Story commits.
- Implementation commits `c5555e4` and `4342d6d` precede final verification/delivery metadata. Final commit/push and PR status are reported in the handoff; human merge remains separate.

## Round 2 — approved pacing and moving aim

The user requested one missile every half second, a target that moves with the car, and adjustable developer settings. Jira TS-148's description was updated to preserve that approved change. Five rounds now launch at 30-tick intervals (0.5 seconds at 60 Hz; two seconds from first to fifth). Each pending round samples the current host-observed position and heading at its launch. Airborne rounds retain their committed trajectories. The single owner-local terrain guide follows current forward aim throughout the volley. There is still no free/manual target selection or shared marker state.

The existing **F1 → Configs → Salvo** section exposes count, cadence, range, launch height, arc, speed, blast radius, damage/falloff, knockback and marker properties. The cadence label explicitly explains `30 = 0.5 seconds`. Existing saved overrides remain authoritative; the standard local settings file had no saved cadence override when inspected. No protocol/catalog changes or TS-148-specific Circus scoring were introduced.
