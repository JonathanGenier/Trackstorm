## Implemented and scope boundary

The existing Weapon registry/category pool includes Machine Gun. Each physical slot captures 500 rounds, shown as 100%; held input fires 25 rounds/second. Only emitted native rays spend ammunition; release and switching retain rounds/cadence. The normal slot lifecycle disposes of exhausted magazines. Host-observed aim and closest-hit terrain/vehicle rays enforce a 10 m maximum. Defaults are 3.5 HP/round, 12 N s central impulse, 3-degree spread, full damage through 5 m and exponent-1.5 falloff to zero at 10 m. Perfect near-range ammunition totals 1750 HP against the hosted 1000 HP baseline; this is tunable rather than health-derived.

Existing authority, complete item publications/checkpoints, configuration, HUD and presentation paths carry ammunition, firing and restoration. Nine tuning controls plus generated spawn weight are in the existing 141-key catalog. Item/configuration wire versions are 11/17. Tracers and small muzzle/hit flashes are presentation-only. Per-round sound reuses a licensed existing sample. No new dependency was acquired.

Damage uses the normal vehicle damage transaction and source machine-gun, instigator player and bullet context. Nonlethal bullet damage awards no Circus points. Lethal damage receives the existing generic kill credit/kill award; no machine-gun-specific or temporary damage-scoring path exists. TS-155 remains the owner of shared item-damage scoring.

## Assumptions, limitations and risks

- Controlled native poses isolate weapon hits from steering/collision noise. They deliberately overwrite motion, so neither screenshots nor the range fixture establish free-body impulse feel. At 3 m centre spacing vehicles can overlap; that observation is only hit/resource evidence.
- Interactive controls toggle production held input and scripted tracking. They do not replace a keyboard/controller driving duel, independent-device latency assessment or competitive balance session. A perfectly aligned large target at 7 m centre separation has its near surface inside the full-damage region.
- Sound assets load, voices execute and cues deduplicate correctly, but no listening/volume/spatialization quality claim is made.
- Local UDP impairment is 30 ms outbound latency, 5 ms jitter and 2% loss. Real authenticated EOS, NAT traversal, Internet play, exported builds and independent physical machines were not exercised in this Story completion run. Recovery identity/lease seams are described by the native harnesses.
- Reliable complete item publications during sustained fire increase traffic. Two firing peers and existing eight-peer item regressions do not prove eight simultaneous machine guns over a constrained Internet link. No long soak or formal frame-time/bandwidth benchmark was performed.
- Future shared TS-155 damage scoring remains intentionally absent. Existing ordinary kill awards remain intact. The 1750 HP theoretical total is an initial balance value, not a promise about human accuracy.
- Ten pre-existing untracked rock texture PNGs were left untouched and excluded from the Story commit.
- No unresolved production defect was found in the exercised scope. Human critique acceptance and the existing independent PR review process remain separate from this evidence; the Story PR is not merged.
