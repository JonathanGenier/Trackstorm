## Implemented publication decisions

- Continuing straight Missile and Salvo flight uses 60 Hz unreliable `TJ` presentation samples, bounded to 668 bytes for sixteen projectiles. A reliable item revision owns the exact projectile membership and arc parameters. Session/revision/tick/identity/lifetime/finite-value/arc validation prevents stale or malformed motion from replacing that boundary. Losing all motion cannot lose a reliable impact, alter damage/inventory, or resurrect a removed entity. Existing visual positions and travel-audio emitters consume motion without replaying one-shot effects.
- Launch, impact, expiry, owner removal, tuning changes and item/resource outcomes retain reliable complete publication. Core still performs every 60 Hz segment sweep and authoritative transaction. Nitro charge, earned overspeed scores, Machine Gun events/ammunition and moving Proxy Mines remain reliable at their existing cadence. These are measured residual costs, not claimed optimizations.
- Only unbanked Circus progress is coalesced to the newest complete state at up to 10 Hz. Committed score, damage/death watermarks, roster, configuration and Game Loop phase transitions bypass that cadence. Earned awards are never discarded or combined across revisions. Local host observation remains every Core revision.
- Join/reconnect and migration continue capturing live complete authority, independently of the most recent ordinary reliable publication. Existing migration cadence, pause/running/vote controls, checkpoint compression/chunking and bounds remain unchanged. Untouched Oil was already event-driven with exact-revision baseline reuse and remains so.
- Vehicle snapshots retain 20 Hz unreliable delivery even on reliable item-publication ticks. Suppressing those apparent duplicates could stall movement behind delayed reliable traffic. Static environment already uses change detection plus a periodic refresh. No broader payload compression, transport replacement or checkpoint-frequency reduction was introduced.

There is no new production queue, per-packet logging or blocking network work. The new packet allocates only bounded projectile data. Verification-only gateway decorators record at most 256 peer/protocol/delivery counters and emit one summary on disposal.

## Native traffic interpretation and limitations

[Native counters](ts-120/native-traffic-summary.json) summarize complete fixture lifecycles across all sending gateways, including client inputs and connection/chunk envelopes. Every native log contains the per-peer, per-protocol, reliable/unreliable message/byte totals and maximum payload. Different fixture durations, render modes, admission timing and unseeded impairment prohibit treating their aggregate byte difference as a controlled bandwidth percentage. The deterministic table above supplies that controlled comparison.

The ordinary native driving fixture showed very little reliable traffic before optimization, unlike the sustained Top Speed deterministic case. Baseline/final complete two-player totals were 736,431 / 732,731 bytes and eight-player totals 10,548,366 / 10,480,557 bytes; no meaningful reduction is claimed for that ordinary driving path. It was already dominated by unchanged unreliable vehicle/environment traffic.

Final eight-player driving at 30 ms outbound delay, 10 ms jitter and 2% loss passed the existing gates: worst peer steady correction 0.802928 m, worst steady p99 0.056398 m, zero hard snaps, aggregate five steady prediction-hold ticks, maximum 33 pending inputs. Baseline values were 0.215385 m, 0.033660 m, zero hard snaps, zero steady holds and maximum 35 pending inputs. These are independent unseeded local runs, not proof of improved movement quality; TS-69 owns that quality work. Input history remains capped at 120 and speculative prediction at 18. Raw per-peer reports are retained beside the logs.

Unverified: real separate-PC authenticated EOS, Internet/NAT/relay paths, cross-platform runtime, exported clean-machine operation, long-session soak, sustained worst-case Oil populations at tuning limits, physical-wire bandwidth/retransmission accounting, native queue-byte peaks and subjective audio listening. The unreliable flight path can hold its last displayed pose during loss; reliable membership/outcomes converge. High-rate exact Nitro resource/score traffic remains an acknowledged cost. This Story does not certify TS-121 soak acceptance.
