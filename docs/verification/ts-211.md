## Implemented

- One deliberate activation commits exactly five automatic launches. Full rack
  and captive-load readiness gates the first activation; early taps are not
  queued. The first release takes five simulation ticks after commitment;
  subsequent launches are 75 ticks (1.25 seconds) apart by default. Developer
  Options supports 30–180 ticks; magazine capacity remains exactly five.
- The existing TS-175 catapult preloads one captive round and displays a looping
  lit fuse without a gameplay fuse timer. Accepted deadlines drive the rapid
  45-degree spoon stroke, winch return, spring motion, feed and ammunition count.
  Regular world snapshots advance the presentation clock between reliable ammo
  events. The stationary lower spring plate and existing authored rig remain intact.
- Shared camera intent, car assistance, cursor, assisted-target indicator and
  existing gold terrain circle serve front, side and rear aim. Each launch takes
  its own accepted direction and current vehicle pose; old arcs remain independent.
  Launch origins match the authored bowl at its release stop. Terrain-circle
  segments crossing a sharp height discontinuity are omitted across both ring edges.
- Missing terrain, out-of-arena aim or expired aim input cannot cancel the volley.
  Missing input falls back to chassis forward. Switch/discard requests cannot
  cancel an accepted or pending magazine, including the pre-step command race.
  Each shot retires its capability. Capacity reservation protects all remaining
  rounds within the shared 16-projectile limit; rejected activation preserves ammo.
- Existing bounded inventory/checkpoint fields retain remaining rounds, the next
  deadline and independent flights. Restore does not replay old events. Death,
  reset, permanent departure and Finished clear applicable continuation. A new
  life cannot resume a destroyed magazine, including retained-inventory respawn policy.

The existing projectile representation, arc collision, damage, scoring and audio
cues remain in use. TS-212 projectile appearance/spin/timed detonation and TS-302
camera overhaul are outside this change. No assets, dependencies, versions,
project settings or GitHub workflow files were changed; Jira was not modified.

[rack](../features/items-rack.md), [items](../features/items.md),
