## Implemented

- Airborne rounds now use the original crimson paper firecracker, ivory bands and
  brass fuse end extracted from the catapult's `Round_0` subtree. Rack, bowl and
  flight share one authored model/material source. The old flying rocket mesh and
  short exhaust emitter are removed from this path.
- Loaded and flying rounds display a bright white spark/glare. The fuse has no
  gameplay timer. Existing individual captive feed, five visible rounds, winch,
  mechanical release, Car rack and deployment remain in use.
- Core starts a fixed 1,800-tick lifetime at launch. The committed parabola
  continues past its aimed point without retargeting. First eligible swept contact
  detonates; otherwise the final lifetime step detonates, including off-map.
  Collision wins on the expiry step. Existing atomic impact/removal owns exactly
  one event/damage transaction. Existing owner-death/departure/reset/Finished
  cancellation still removes associated rounds without a new explosion.
- Recovery and motion validation retain launch age and validate analytic position
  and velocity, including late off-map flight. No new packet layout or authority
  owner was introduced. Core remains engine-independent.
- Client adds deterministic axial spin/tumble, at most 100 ms extrapolation and
  one arena-owned world-space smoke pool. Its 16,384 segments cover 60 Hz emission
  per round with six-step catch-up. Segments drift/grow and fade through age 15
  seconds, surviving projectile removal. Saturation declines new smoke instead of
  deleting old segments early; late admission does not replay smoke history.
- Added deterministic lifetime/contact/recovery cases and a rendered production
  model/smoke fixture in `check-salvo.ps1`. Updated recovery and EMP blast fixtures
  to use valid continuation and actual physical contact.

TS-211's five-shot controller, per-launch camera snapshot and tuning remain intact.
TS-213 blast redesign and TS-214 final integration/balance were not implemented.
No Jira, versions, dependencies, project settings or GitHub workflows changed.
The user authorized normal push with existing PR automation; no manual PR creation
is part of this delivery.
