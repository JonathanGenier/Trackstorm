Branch `ts-317-pg`, based on main `e2315a2f7bfcab3102857ec168557c8445af52da`.
Read TS-317, TS-313 and completed TS-314–316; all returned no comments, and TS-317 has no subtasks. The recommended model is GPT-6 Astra — High; this implementation uses GPT-6 Astra. No Jira changes.

- Existing native/Core segment arbitration already denies EMP on mounted/deployed armor and routes Machine Gun hits to Shield HP. These paths are retained and regression-tested.
- Missile/Salvo blast victims now test live mounted and deployed panels in world space. The mounted pose uses the current authoritative observation, including pitch/roll; deployed walls use accepted captured geometry.
- Resolve all vehicle and EMP-nose exposure before damaging any armor. A lethal hit protects through that explosion; later impacts in the same batch see the removed pool. Armor damage is once per explosion, independent of the number/order of cars.
- Physical chassis exposure uses the nearest point, face/edge projections and vertices of the existing convex movement hull. Salvo uses existing falloff at the nearest exposed candidate; Missile retains vehicle-center falloff. No fixed damage split or new multiplier. Fully covered cars receive neither HP damage nor blast impulse. Direct mounted-panel impacts retain complete protection for that car.
- Armor uses its nearest physical point for radial damage, including Missile wing hits; this corrects the prior dependence on the protected car/wall center. World-wall impulse keeps its existing calculation. Stored shields are excluded. The common atomic health/destruction/slot/replay path is unchanged.
- Blast queries advance their start by 2 mm to avoid treating an outward ray beginning on a panel face as penetration. The same rule covers EMP membranes.
- Mine protection uses the current native contact position and nearest chassis point, including live deployed panels. Mine damage remains contact-only; its cinematic dust does not gain radial damage or additional victims.
- Added 16 deterministic cases and an impaired three-arena native mode/script, plus automatic verification routing. Updated the old overlapping Salvo fixture to assert partial upper-hull exposure rather than its obsolete no-cross-cover rule.
- Preserved TS-314 geometry, TS-315 support fitting and TS-316 mass, contact damage, torque, stability and cached terrain-fit behavior. No new assets, dependencies, packet layouts or Shared layer.
- Repository-required normal Story version: `0.2.67`; Windows preset `0.2.67.0`.

## Runtime evidence and fixture boundaries

The new native fixture uses three production NetworkVehicleArena instances over real local GNS/UDP sockets with 60 ms outbound delay, 12 ms jitter and 3% simulated loss. It checks Missile/Salvo front, rear and upper-rear explosions for unrotated and yawed/pitched cars, independent HP and armor-sequence convergence, simultaneous EMP impacts on the rear plate and both wings, rear/front mine contacts, a damaged deployed wall's lethal hit, a subsequent chassis hit, and 600 further physics frames without resurrection. The rear mine spends exactly 250 shield HP with no car loss; the front mine spends exactly 250 car HP with no armor loss.

Projectile/mine starting states, fixed car poses, health restoration between cases and the installed world-wall pose are explicit fixtures. Native collision, authority transactions, replication and presentation use production paths. A small native obstacle locates external blasts; the broad five-metre Salvo fixture deliberately exposes armor and chassis to one blast. Default-radius regressions remain in the Core Salvo tests and the ordinary native Salvo fixture.

Upper-rear Salvo smoke can obscure the car/armor in the saved view; damage assertions, rather than that screenshot alone, establish which HP pools changed.

## Limitations and remaining risks

- Exposure is a bounded set of paths on the physical convex hull, not an exact continuous integration of visible surface area. Extremely narrow openings between sampled paths can remain classified as cover. Cosmetics and tires are not separate blast targets. No fixed percentage is used.
- Automated local peers and simulated latency do not establish authenticated EOS, real Internet latency, independent physical PCs, physical-controller ergonomics or human competitive balance.
- The new fixture fixes car poses to isolate geometry; moving-fire and native heavy-contact scenarios are separate regressions. Full weapon-by-direction-by-elevation combinations during unconstrained human driving remain for playtesting.
- Historical TS-316 generic item/Nitro/reconnect harness failures are not claimed fixed by this Story. Old test results are not counted as current evidence.
