## Direct runtime evidence and iteration findings

- Actual basin collision poses: 574 shallow and 130 deep immersion samples in the test grid. The three Mud basins remained dry even for below-terrain poses. Above-water jumps were dry; inverted/subterrain Water poses remained hazardous without support rays.
- Both production adapters: three stationary deep-water deaths/respawns each. At 90 ticks, HP was 625 and immersion approximately 1.209 m; ordinary death and full-HP new-life respawn followed.
- Both adapters: three full-throttle shallow long-axis exits each, zero HP loss and zero remaining immersion; scripted driving from the bank crossed shallow then deep Water.
- The first shallow-exit probe faced the steeper short-side bank and did not escape from rest over four seconds (HP remained 1000). A long-axis probe advanced but had not cleared the tire footprint in four seconds. Ten-second long-axis runs consistently exited. This limitation was documented, not hidden by changing production tuning or moving the driven vehicle during the scenario.
- Eight real local UDP peers with 30 ms outbound delay, 5 ms jitter and 2% loss completed four remote water deaths and respawns. Every peer agreed on Dead/Respawning/Alive, authoritative deadlines/spawns/HP, inactivity, item cleanup, new-life state and once-per-life bounded presentation cleanup.
- The first impaired lifecycle runs failed a pre-existing startup assumption: world state arrived before reliable item state (`pickups=0/null`, music active). The fixture now awaits item state and then runs the original map/pickup assertions. Failed-run shutdown leak messages were confined to those aborted runs; later passing runs are independently reported.
- The first full infield run rejected the first 4.1667 HP of water damage during an obsolete damage-free Water recovery expectation. The updated fixture preserves all basin geometry probes and limits damage-free basin recovery to non-Water terrain; dedicated water tests cover the intentional hazard.
- Rendered Godot viewport captures were inspected: the shared-field waterline fills the designated pond, the vehicle roof remains visible during deep immersion, and surrounding Mud basins retain their prior appearance. This is functional presentation, not final water VFX.
- Windows computer-use capture was attempted twice but failed with `SetIsBorderRequired failed: No such interface supported (0x80004002)`. Godot viewport capture succeeded. No manual keyboard/controller driving or listening result is claimed.

## Assumptions, limitations and explicitly unverified areas

- Water authoring assumes upright, unit-scale terrain and a horizontal waterline. No buoyancy, wave simulation, underwater camera effect, splash/wake polish or dedicated water sound is included.
- Immersion uses five footprint samples and nominal tire-level offset. Ordinary native entry/exit, inverted poses and below-terrain gaps are covered; arbitrary teleports between ticks and extreme externally injected speeds are not swept-volume guarantees.
- Shallow Water is deliberately more restrictive than Deep Mud. The gentler long-axis exit is verified; not every short-side bank can be climbed from a standstill. Human judgement of driving feel and gamepad ergonomics remains unverified.
- Networking evidence is local native UDP and existing recovery harnesses. Authenticated EOS, exported builds on separate physical machines, Internet conditions and indefinite endurance are unverified.
- Water-specific checkpoint retention is directly covered in Core; generic native reconnect/migration coverage does not independently establish a real Internet disconnect while submerged.
- Two pre-existing edits (`code/Core/Input/InputButtons.cs` whitespace and `assets/maps/oval/grass_variation.png.import`) were preserved and are excluded from Story delivery.
