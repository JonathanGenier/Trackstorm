## Implemented behavior

- Default Salvo blast radius is 0.9144 m, with the existing DevTools/configuration
  path allowing 0.1–30 m. Damage, impulse, falloff, firing cadence, launch physics,
  aiming, catapult mechanics and the existing 1,800-tick launch lifetime are retained.
- `SalvoExplosion` presents one authoritative detonation as three token-seeded
  colorful flashes/spark bursts 0.16 seconds apart. Twenty smoke billboards expand
  and fade to expiry at 20 seconds. These effects never create gameplay events.
- The existing atomic projectile impact/removal transaction remains the sole
  damage source. Salvo damage reaches the nearest physical chassis, shield/wall
  or rock extent. The shared chassis vertices preserve the existing convex native
  collision shape and raised bumper undercuts. Impulse direction remains outward
  toward the body center. Missile blast behavior is unchanged.
- Existing attached-shield center-path coverage is evaluated before armor damage,
  including the lethal shield hit. Exposed front bodywork remains vulnerable.
  Independently exposed bodywork and nearby armor can each receive one application;
  shields do not grant new immunity to other vehicles. Detached walls receive one
  application per blast regardless of nearby car count.
- Existing attached EMP membrane damage and shield cover remain authoritative;
  pops do not cause secondary blasts. Stored EMP ammunition remains Missile-only.
- Imported intact rocks provide oriented mesh bounds through matching-build layout
  data, without new wire state. Existing staged destruction and deadlines consume
  the same committed explosion once. Broken pieces use the imported debris box,
  normalized to the existing size profile and transformed by piece yaw/offset.
  A large rotated debris edge has its own native impact regression.
- Reliable effect revisions reject duplicate publications and rebase on an empty
  accepted recovery checkpoint. Re-detonation of a restored round with live local
  smoke keeps its existing owner instead of duplicating or restarting the effect.
  The arena bounds explosion owners at 128 and
  smoke billboards at 2,560. Saturation retires the oldest cosmetic owner early.
- Added a durable explosion runtime harness and fast-check routing; synchronized
  Salvo, shield, shared item, destruction and asset/provenance documentation.
