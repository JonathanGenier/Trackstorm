## Implemented behavior and systems

- Native `WheelSuspension` supplies exact individual support points to the existing Core observations. Unsupported tires and chassis-only overlap do not trigger Oil. Core evaluates circular/capsule footprint pieces against these support points, including banked surfaces.
- `OilTractionState` retains four independent timers. An eligible entry applies five seconds of full impairment, followed by two seconds of smoothstep-shaped recovery by default. Continuous overlap ages normally through complete recovery; a distinct wheel re-entry refreshes only that wheel.
- Existing per-tire forces retain steering, countersteering, acceleration, braking, suspension and momentum. Front Oil reduces lateral purchase; rear Oil reduces longitudinal and lateral purchase. Split grip generates ordinary lever-arm torque. Oil adds no scripted yaw, heading change or control disable. Low-speed handbrake holding respects the contaminated lateral/longitudinal budgets.
- One logical `OilPatch` owns every segment from one activation, the owner, absolute deadline, consumed contacts and shared quota. Defaults are two successful vehicle contacts or 30 seconds. Multiple simultaneous wheels/pieces consume one vehicle contact. Death/reset/rejected batches cannot commit an unapplied contact. Recovery no longer releases an overlapping vehicle's latch.
- The existing TS-156 authoritative Circus path awards 50 base points multiplied by the retained owner's current K/D for eligible rival entries. Own contact consumes quota without offensive points. No parallel score state or client hit claims were added.
- `OilFootprint` copies/value-compares up to 63 additional circle/capsule segments. Presentation reconstructs all pieces under one scene-tree owner and removes them together. Production emission remains a single native-projected puddle. Its placement now clears the rear tires; the old centre-based offset would immediately catch those tires under the new detection.
- Movement v13 is 169 bytes, including four two-byte timers. World protocol v16, item v24 and configuration v49 reject incompatible layouts. Item/checkpoint state includes shared geometry, quota and current wheel masks. Existing prediction, complete restore, reconnect and authority replacement paths carry the state.
- DevTools uses its existing allowlist, validation, Apply/Cancel/Reset, persistence and shared configuration path. Stats exposes remaining FL/FR/RL/RR contamination.

Primary changed areas: `code/Core/Items`, `code/Core/Vehicles`, `code/Core/Development`, existing world serialization; Client native suspension, Oil presentation/placement and Stats; Core Oil/serialization/default tests; existing native Oil/recovery/handling fixtures; new `check-oil-handling.ps1` and its fast-check route; affected feature documents.
