# TS-320 — Machine Gun production texture integration

## Scope and implementation

Implemented on `ts-320-pg`, based on `d4d2bf8803d8cc50c30a9cc40f5763641b6b32ac`.
Read TS-320, all six acceptance criteria, attachments 10164/10165, and the exact
user-supplied `back 1.png` / `front 1.png` local images. Jira had no children or
comments. Its recommended model remains 5.1 Sol / Medium; this session used
Astra instead, without changing that Jira recommendation.

Original vector artwork supplies red/charcoal/cream diamonds, scrollwork,
scalloped trim and gold stars. A rest-coordinate shader maps the artwork onto
the existing receivers, shrouds, cheeks, cradle and both lower drum magazines,
with brass accents and procedural chips/roughness. Cylindrical mapping wraps
the shell surfaces; planar mapping covers receiver faces. The atlas import
retains mipmaps, and the match loader preloads both skin resources.

The GLB, cartridge GLB, Blender master and geometry authoring script retain
their recorded SHA-256 hashes. Separate animated ammunition belts retain their
materials. No Core behavior, vehicle material, rack support geometry, aiming,
damage, network protocol or firing implementation changed. Versions were not
changed, as explicitly instructed. Added five close-view captures to the
existing rendered fixture; updated feature/provenance documentation.

## Independent Astra runtime critique — Story Round 1

**Overall Score: 6.0 / 10 — BELOW TARGET (<8.0).**

Independent Astra review compared both approved images with the final runtime
captures and reviewed execution logs. This was recorded-evidence inspection,
not the reviewer's own live playtest.

VERIFIED by image inspection: circus identity, both patterned drum shells,
gold rims/dark faces, distinguishable cartridges and normal chase readability.
Recorded runtime evidence supports functional integration; absence of a
material-related regression is INFERRED within those scenarios, not universal.

1. **High — palette and ornament fidelity.** Rear/oval captures show bright
   pink/red scroll panels and pale diamonds compared with the reference's deep
   worn red, aged cream and charcoal framing. Front receiver diamond coverage
   differs from the reference's dark/star emphasis. This weakens reproduction
   of the approved appearance. Suggested correction: match painted proportions
   and motif placement face by face, then tune response under production light.
2. **Medium — weathering/material depth.** Close rear evidence shows broadly
   distributed dark flecks across paint and trim, limited edge/contact wear
   differentiation and comparatively flat gold. Suggested correction: vary
   chip sizes/substrate and localize abrasion, with differentiated paint/brass/
   bare-metal roughness.
3. **Medium — incomplete inspection framing.** Side captures crop most of the
   receivers and drums; top capture largely frames the vehicle and crops the
   weapon. Full wraparound seams/orientation and complete drum ends therefore
   remain insufficiently demonstrated. Suggested correction: reframe and settle
   the camera, capturing complete front/rear/left/right/top and individual drums.

All three findings are **In Scope / Existing Task** corrective work. The
recommended next round, **only if authorized**, prioritizes palette/layout,
material wear and complete inspection coverage while preserving geometry and
behavior. Still images do not establish lack of shimmer or frame-time spikes;
VFX CPU timing is not a shader GPU benchmark. No live independent play,
authenticated EOS/WAN, multi-device or sustained GPU validation was performed.

No further polish or corrective implementation was made after this critique.
The skin is delivered for human direction with the above visual acceptance gaps;
it is not represented as meeting the 8.0 target or receiving human approval.
