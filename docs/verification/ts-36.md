- VERIFIED by diff: Core, collider sizes, mass, collision calculations, movement tuning, prediction and interpolation algorithms are unchanged. No Shared layer was added.
- VERIFIED visually: gameplay-distance practice, airborne explosion and multiplayer captures show the converted model. Existing combat color feedback now uses the roof identification panel.

## Limitations and human checks

- No separate-PC/EOS/WAN gameplay, long-session soak, export validation or GPU benchmark was performed.
- Automated driving and inspected screenshots establish attachment and readability, not human art-direction approval.
- Fixed wheels intentionally cannot follow suspension travel; their contact appearance varies as the physical chassis rises or tilts. The roof exceeds the one-metre collider height, as the previous cabin did.
- Manual acceptance should assess the final wasteland silhouette, identification-panel visibility and visual feel during normal play. No mechanical animation is planned by this change.
