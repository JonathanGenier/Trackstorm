## Limits and evidence interpretation

- Combined-load projectile flight is a committed-event fixture seam; destruction still
  uses production authority and presentation. Separate native/UDP checks cover weapons
  and replication. One local eight-car world is not eight physical players.
- Fixed-FPS harness intervals include scheduling/rendering and are machine-specific;
  they are not hardware-wide frame-rate guarantees or isolated GPU timings.
- Physical gamepad ergonomics, listening/audio judgment, Internet/EOS multi-device play,
  and a fresh packaged/exported executable are unverified in this Story.
- No production map defect was established by the two corrected fixture failures.
  A side-climb line deliberately driven through a visible solid rock is not a safe route;
  the authored rock remains collidable and the collision matrix retains impact coverage.
- Production defaults remain independent of temporary runtime overrides. Fixtures use
  their own explicit configuration; no user host/player settings were reset or edited.
