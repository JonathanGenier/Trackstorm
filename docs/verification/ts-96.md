Verified on 2026-09-19 on `ts-96-jg`, for PR #48. Scope is the approved logical-navigation regression fix and compact Configs layout; TS-97/TS-98/TS-99 redesigns are excluded. Logical directions move between focusable controls, including out of LineEdit and SpinBox's inner editor. Unbound text input still reaches native numeric editing; Accept in a numeric field does not Apply. Cancel/Pause closes only DevTools, restores prior focus, and leaves Settings' independent input suppression intact. F1/F2/F3 retain direct in-place routing.

Configs has a centered area capped at 640 pixels. Grids use a wrapping 280-pixel label column, 16-pixel separation and a 150-pixel value column; labels do not expand across the viewport. Vertical scrolling follows focus at small resolutions.

## Limits and diagnostic observations

- Synthetic native input verifies bindings and focus behavior, not physical-controller ergonomics.
- Multiplayer evidence uses same-machine UDP. Separate-machine authenticated EOS sessions were not exercised.
- Debug rendered runtime and Release compilation were verified; no new exported Release executable was produced or exercised.
- Initial headless menu shutdown sometimes reported retained audio playback resources despite passing assertions. Verbose output identified MP3/Ogg/WAV playback resources. Allowing 100 ms after scene teardown produced clean final headless and rendered menu runs.
- The supplemental headless startup harness also intermittently reported two ObjectDB instances at exit after its assertions passed. A verbose rerun was clean and the rendered startup harness passed without warnings. No broader startup/audio redesign was made; a universally clean headless startup shutdown is not established by this correction.
- Screenshots do not establish audible quality or physical OS/controller behavior on other hardware.
