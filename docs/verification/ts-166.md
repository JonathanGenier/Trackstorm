## Implemented

- Supported front Dirt contacts reserve lateral traction for the filtered wheel direction during slides. Allocation remains inside the existing tire budget, including braking.
- Smooth, traction-bounded dirt yaw recovery makes countersteering arrest a slide instead of allowing prolonged rotation. Handbrake engagement reduces the assist, retaining deliberate drift initiation. No heading snap, velocity replacement, additional propulsion or new serialized vehicle memory.
- Production defaults: `vehicle.dirt_steering_reserve = 0.65` and `vehicle.dirt_recovery = 4/s`. Both use the existing Configs catalog, validated host transaction, Apply/Cancel/Reset, file persistence, reliable replication and checkpoint composition. Configuration wire version is 17, with 133 keys / 1085 bytes; saved files missing the new keys receive defaults.
- Asphalt and other surfaces retain their original force path. Dirt grip, drag, acceleration and power-slip defaults remain unchanged. No map, suspension, collision, presentation or vehicle-architecture redesign.
- Added deterministic recovery, symmetry, no-snap, restoration, unsupported-front, airborne/zero-grip, exact other-surface trajectory, straight dirt acceleration and tuning validation/effect coverage. Updated materially affected feature documentation.
- Pre-existing `InputButtons.cs` formatting and ten untracked rock texture files were preserved and excluded from this delivery.

### Baseline versus final dirt recovery

Same flat Dirt setup: start at 18 m/s; accelerate for 120 ticks to 31.21 m/s; apply 0.75 steering, full throttle and handbrake for 75 ticks; release handbrake with full opposite steering and 0.65 throttle for 60 ticks; then neutral steering with 0.65 throttle for 120 ticks.

| Observation | Original implementation | Final implementation |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| After initiation: lateral speed | -21.14 m/s | -19.74 m/s |
| After initiation: yaw | -1.96 rad/s | -1.49 rad/s |
| After countersteer: longitudinal speed | -14.49 m/s (travelling backward relative to body) | +21.41 m/s |
| After countersteer: road speed | 14.81 m/s | 22.96 m/s |
| After neutral recovery: road speed | 1.14 m/s | 31.45 m/s |
| After neutral recovery: lateral speed / yaw | approximately zero after slowing almost to rest | 0.029 m/s / 0.0075 rad/s |

Final initiation retained power-slip memory 0.2197 and visible curved tire tracks/dust. Countersteer changed the heading trajectory progressively, and neutral input settled the car without a speed collapse. Final post-verification critique replay reproduced these final values. Evidence: `baseline-*.json`, `final-*.json`, `critique-slide/counter/settle.json` and corresponding critique PNGs.

**VERIFIED:** Ordinary cornering at 0.45 steering/0.75 throttle from 15 m/s ended at 23.75 m/s, with 2.62 m/s lateral slip and 0.123 power slip. Low-speed initiation from 9 m/s, opposite steering and recovery remained upright at 1000 HP. Repeated alternating drifts settled after two seconds of neutral steering to lateral speeds -0.040 and +0.015 m/s, yaw -0.011 and +0.0035 rad/s, and speeds 30.67 and 36.94 m/s. The first candidate's lingering powered yaw tail was corrected before formal critique and the affected checks were rerun.

**VERIFIED:** Native Asphalt ordinary cornering and straightening were exercised separately: no power slip, 30.80 m/s after the corner and 42.16 m/s after acceleration/straightening, with essentially zero residual yaw/lateral motion. Deterministic regression tests require exact equality with both new assists disabled over 180 ticks on Asphalt and five unrelated surfaces. The native vehicle and oval suites provide broader accepted-handling regression evidence.

### Kicker to bridge/tabletop

**VERIFIED:** West/east native jump harnesses passed their controlled 14/16/18 m/s target approaches and damage-free route completion. West 18 m/s target produced a 14.71 m/s horizontal launch, 22.24 m airborne travel and landing at X=-58.13, followed by controlled progress to X=-27.40. Those slower checks alone do not establish a landing near the central bridge.

**VERIFIED:** Additional rendered input-driven rolling approaches started at X=-125, Z=0, facing +X, with ordinary 20 and 22 m/s initial speed. Full throttle carried the car to the kicker; throttle was reduced to 0.7 during flight. No boost, aerial force, teleport after setup or map/tuning override was used.

- 20 m/s rolling approach: horizontal flight speed 23.94 m/s, first supported landing X=-14.01, Z=-0.56; continued across bridge center to X=0.28 under control.
- 22 m/s rolling approach: horizontal flight speed 24.70 m/s, first supported landing X=-7.37, Z=-2.36 on Concrete near the central bridge; continued to X=17.69 at 28.72 m/s. The bridge center is X=0. This directly establishes approximately-middle reach with an ordinary rolling approach.
- These faster probes lost about 5.57 and 10.79 of 1000 HP during takeoff; no additional HP was lost at their supported landings. The source of that small takeoff loss was not isolated. Suspension/collision/map changes were outside this focused implementation.
- A standing start at X=-125 also completed the jump, landing at X=-35.80. Exploratory starts farther west did not establish a clean route: X=-155 met an obstacle/grass and lost HP; an uncorrected X=-135 approach drifted toward the tabletop edge. Those probes are not counted as successful normal approaches or hidden as passes.
