## Assumptions, limitations and unverified areas

- The 73-impact sequence validates the Client's visual bounds through synthetic accepted publications, not 73 consecutive authoritative gameplay detonations. Ten seconds of continued runtime checks burst cleanup. Scar expiry is advanced to the 60-second age; a real-time 60-second render soak of 64 scars was not performed.
- Local direct-IP/UDP peers and a trusted identity test seam do not establish authenticated EOS, separate-device or WAN behavior. Impaired visual smoothness, reconnect during the moving arm, and rendered migration transitions remain unverified.
- The prototype-map fixture does not show the full production combat HUD or a normal free-driving Oval match. Other cameras, lighting, weather, renderers/GPUs, exported builds and continuous competitive play are unverified.
- No quantitative GPU frame-time or VRAM measurement was taken. The 10-second sustained presentation sequence establishes bounded node lifetime and absence of observed runtime errors in this renderer, not a performance benchmark.
