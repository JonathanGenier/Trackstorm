# TS-74 — Infield layout graybox verification

## Scope and implementation

Jira TS-74 was read in full, including its attachment, dependencies, empty child
list and empty comments. Its current description already incorporates the approved
motocross/off-road intent. Attachment 10017, `Map Concept 0.2.0 Scale.png`, was
visually inspected in Jira at overview and enlarged central-junction views.
It supplied layout relationships only, never authoritative dimensions.
Repository workflow, standards, critique rules, feature index, oval/vehicle
authoring notes, asset registry and production implementation were inspected first.

The editable `assets/maps/infield/source/InfieldGraybox.blend` and GLB add two
side loops, a north connector, lateral shortcuts, a north/south spine, six oval
access points and three open junction areas. Ten routes have 12–16 m driving
widths and 4 m shoulders. Four water footprints, three obstacle areas and two
rhythm stretches are reserved. Each of two lateral jump corridors allocates
30 m approach, 12 m kicker, 13 m flight footprint, 22 m dirt landing and 23 m
recovery at 12 m width. The central tunnel envelope has 18 m clear width and
5.5 m ceiling clearance. Twenty-metre transition reservations along both
straights remain free of new solid boundaries.

Blender retains editable footprint geometry and bakes its flat colors onto one
visual floor to avoid coplanar overlay flicker. Godot imports five Blender tunnel
solids with collision. Existing flat infield collision supplies route support;
the diagram is 5 cm above it. No final terrain, jump profiles, water behavior,
materials, dressing or handling changes are implemented. Oval geometry/collision,
eight grid spawns, 20 pickup markers and the 4.81 m production car are preserved.

The branch was synchronized with `origin/main` at `3f36d44`, including TS-117,
before final verification. Story version is `0.1.32`, Windows versions `0.1.32.0`.
An existing unrelated whitespace edit in `code/Core/Input/InputButtons.cs` was
preserved and is not TS-74 work.

## Runtime observations

Every final individual route and jump footprint remained grounded with zero
unsupported frames and no collision damage. The continuous loop tour recorded
3.50 m maximum centerline error within its 12 m lane. Both cars completed their
separated tunnel paths without damage. Logical inputs drove actual production
`VehicleBody` instances through Core simulation and native Godot physics. Cars
were initialized at scenario starts, never teleported along their routes.

The [overview](ts-74/overview.png), [tunnel](ts-74/tunnel.png),

[two-car approach](ts-74/TwoCarTunnel-drive.png) were directly inspected. They

removed the earlier striped overlay artifacts.

## Limitations, risks and explicitly unverified areas

- This validates flat topology. Future jump trajectories, landing impacts,
  elevations, berms, rhythm physics and terrain transitions need TS-75 work and
  new verification. Reserved dimensions do not prove later profiles at any speed.
- The tunnel is an open-sided clearance envelope. Earth cover, elevated crossing
  and climbable approaches are not implemented or validated.
- Native steering targets roughly 6–10 m/s. Competitive-speed infield balance,
  sustained high-speed infield driving and human steering feel were not tested.
  Original oval high-speed driving was tested separately.
- Every route was driven and the major loops joined in one continuous tour.
  A single uninterrupted run visiting every branch and every intersection turn
  was not recorded. Opposing traffic and dense multi-car infield racing were not
  tested; the two-car tunnel scenario is limited coverage.
- Human interactive driving was not performed by the agent. On 2026-09-23 the
  user reported "human test passed" and authorized PR creation; the exact manual
  scenarios were not supplied. Audio listening, authenticated EOS, remote Internet
  peers, independent devices and exported builds were not tested by the agent.
- Screenshots use a verification camera. Existing replication camera checks
  passed, but tunnel chase-camera occlusion was not independently characterized.
- No performance benchmark is claimed. Builds/checks overlapped, and replication
  logs include frame stalls under that workload.
