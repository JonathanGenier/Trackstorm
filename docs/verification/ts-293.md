### Changes and cause

- Moved all three contacts from vehicle z=-1.75 m to -1.98 m, 23 cm forward and
  14 cm behind the bonnet's front edge. Recomputed height on the sloping bonnet;
  lateral positions remain -0.50/0/+0.50 m. Sparks still originate at the actual
  drill tip with the unchanged effect. The default sequence remains 0.96 seconds.
- Preserved the approved model and original rest/deploy/stow path. Forward repair
  travel blends an additional 10 cm of translation in each existing telescopic
  section. This is necessary to retain forearm clearance over the roof lamps at
  the farther contact, without stretching meshes or modifying the asset.
- Cause of cancellation: `CarRackPresentation.Observe` treated a concurrent grant
  or selection as a new acquisition and cleared pending use/repair state. The HUD
  independently displayed immediate authoritative depletion before the mechanism
  returned. Event matching also depended on the latest selected capability rather
  than the installed arm's capability.
- An accepted Wrench use now owns the physical rack through all three contacts,
  arm nesting, rack lowering and complete trunk closure. Incoming accepted
  inventory/selection is retained for the subsequent handoff, not allowed to reset
  that lifecycle. Matching the installed capability preserves the use across an
  overlapping inventory update. Owner and observer use the same rack path.
- The production HUD reads the rack's installed-capability projection for the
  retiring physical slot. It exposes empty or the incoming item only when the
  actual mechanism releases that slot. There is no HUD timer. Local Use and
  Discard cannot operate on an incoming capability hidden behind this handoff;
  switches remain accepted as pending intent.
- Core still consumes/heals immediately and accepts pickups into available
  inventory. The projected spent Wrench is not restored to authority and cannot
  heal twice. No healing amount, pickup rule, serialization or packet changed.
  Death/life reset/reseed retain their existing immediate cleanup behavior.
