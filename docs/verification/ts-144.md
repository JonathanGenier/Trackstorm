## Direct runtime evidence

VERIFIED: both native practice driving and the actual hosted collision adapter
complete the seven approaches through logical input. Scenario setup resets the
vehicle; traversal never teleports or injects launch velocity. All successful
routes remain dry and finish with 1000/1000 production HP. Practice paths use
the existing steering helper; hosted paths repeat them independently.

The water-edge route runs along Z=-43 at a 12 m/s target: minimum center-to-marker
distance 0.880 m practice / 0.879 m hosted and zero immersion throughout. A poor
inward approach at X=77 acquires the pickup then reaches 1.002 m actual immersion
near Z=-38.28. This is real WaterObservation exposure, not merely a basin-distance
test. Excess-speed, sideways collision and combat-push variants are not exhaustively tested.

At 14/16/18 m/s approach targets, the west jump acquires the elevated pickup
airborne through both adapters. Hosted nearest distances are 2.169/1.377/1.164 m,
inside the unchanged 3 m sphere. Practice ordinary crossings at 6 and 10 m/s stay
4.095/4.137 m away; hosted slow crossing and a 14 m/s tabletop-only approach stay
4.095/4.094 m away and never acquire it. Both underpass exclusion runs reject it.
Practice jump cases additionally verify actual airborne travel, tabletop landing,
grounded recovery and no collision damage.

VERIFIED: eight local UDP peers receive all 27 marker IDs and states. Two remote
players contest the first infield pickup; exactly one gets a matching claimant,

locations include all seven infield pickups and the first oval single. Full slots
do not consume availability or overwrite grants. Thirty-two additional rounds per

## Limitations and unverified areas

- Human keyboard/controller feel, live human combat route preference and sustained
  pickup balance remain UNVERIFIED. Automated driving is not human playtesting.
- Remote devices, authenticated EOS/NAT, exported builds and Internet latency
  remain UNVERIFIED; native UDP checks use local processes/worlds.
- No FPS/performance budget or long-session stress result is claimed.
  The separate-process headless network run recorded frame stalls (client maximum
  701.679 ms, P99 98.632 ms; host maximum 3287.648 ms, P99 177.841 ms) despite passing convergence; client prediction error
  P99 was 0.476 m and maximum 2.338 m. No causal attribution to these seven markers
  is established, and this run does not prove smooth rendered multiplayer.
- Custom host pickup-radius tuning can remove the elevated skill requirement;
  the layout intentionally targets unchanged default gameplay. Arbitrary speeds,
  vehicle tuning, blast trajectories and every recovery angle are not covered.
- Existing slope-start limitations and TS-82's accepted presentation/performance
  limitations remain outside scope. Geometry was preserved rather than redesigned.
- No Jira closure, PR, merge or human acceptance is claimed.
