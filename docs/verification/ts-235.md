### Implemented and verified contract

- Core now carries an actual shot ordinal in each magazine and reliable round
  outcome. Left and right alternate distinct real rounds: default **80 rounds/s
  total, 40 per gun**, one ammo unit per round. Default 800-round depletion,
  deterministic spread, shared aim, damage, falloff, protected pivot-to-range hit
  queries and accepted endpoints remain intact. Both guns receive real damaging
  outcomes; no additional ray or damage is invented by presentation.
- Replaced inferred magazine parity with that explicit round identity for bullets,
  tracers, feed/recoil and muzzle feedback, including final depletion and recovery.
  Each gun owns its own heat and firing age. Flash and smoke are placed exactly
  at the corresponding articulated muzzle endpoint and stop on release/readiness loss.
- Fixed the every-other-round tracer phase lock that put all bright tracers on
  the left gun. The staggered per-barrel selection preserves default 50% overall
  density. Default selected tracer mix is 80% existing warm color, 10% red and
  10% green in the exercised sample, with all three on both barrels. Ordinary
  bullets retain their existing subdued color and treatment.
- Added bounded ammo cost (1–10 units per real round, default 1) to the existing
  shared configuration path. Live cost edits preserve actual shot order; a final
  partial cost consumes the remaining units and exhausts the item. Capacity
  changes affect future pickups only. Two item slots remain independent.
- Existing Dev Options now explain aggregate cadence, range, scatter, capacity,
  usage and tracer interval. A searchable local VFX accordion adds nine controls
  for speed, lengths, width, brightness, red/green mix, flash and smoke. Bullet
  tuning is captured per new emission; muzzle tuning is live. Local values are
  session-only, neither exported nor replicated. Production gameplay defaults remain.
- Retained 512 total / 64-per-shooter flights, fixed maximum 550 ms lifetime and
  no per-round scene nodes, meshes or materials. Cosmetic tuning cannot change
  hit results or these allocation ceilings. Extreme tuning/catch-up may drop VFX.
- Item wire TI33 and configuration TC56 (285 values / 2301 bytes) carry the new
  counters/usage. Online compatibility bucket is `trackstorm-lobby-25`; older
  layouts are rejected. No new assets, dependencies, impact work or HUD work.
