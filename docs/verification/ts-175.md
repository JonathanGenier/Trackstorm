  study, game GLB, seven original grain/patina materials, provenance/hashes and
  a read-only geometry/rig audit under `assets/items/salvo`.

  rim, forward sliding full-yaw bearing, mechanical stop buffers, telescoping
  spring support, compressed coil and visible winch/drum inside the production rack.

  through the bearing axis to the spoon underside. No duplicate vehicle rack.
- One flat row of five horizontal pyrotechnics on the spoon handle, with captive
  escapement and bowl retainers. The loaded round moves along the handle into
  the bowl in vehicle-local coordinates; inverted poses do not drop ammunition.

  Catapult extension starts only at full rack height. Return centers yaw, finishes
  release, nests the assembly and then permits rack lowering/trunk closure.

  45-degree stop/recoil, winch-reset and reload presentation. Remaining rounds do
  not respawn during ordinary five-round consumption.
- Reuses the validated accepted aim channel for yaw. Salvo remains outside
  direct-fire sticky assistance and keeps its existing local ground guide.
  Accepted shot-token succession preserves turret heading while old-token input
  stays invalid. Recovery reconstructs current ownership without replaying shots.

  focused system/asset documentation. Version properties and export presets remain
