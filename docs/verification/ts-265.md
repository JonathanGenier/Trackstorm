## Implemented

- Permanent tire/underside landing immunity, including bottom-out and partial-wheel support; bumper contacts outside the wheel footprint remain damaging at steep attitudes.
- Terrain body-contact continuation coalesces persistent manifold points and re-arms separate hits without the general 12-tick cooldown. Aggregate v5 / world protocol v14 retain this memory.
- Core crash continuation suppresses driving, Nitro thrust and air-control input/stabilization until the first wheel contact. Ordinary air controls remain unchanged.
- Shared dissipative terrain-body impulse response retains nose-over and rolling momentum in practice and network sweeps. Native solving still supplies the pose.
- Automatic assistance begins after 0.5 s, ramps over 0.35 s, continues low-energy rotation toward tires and introduces no linear launch impulse or pose reset. Existing host tuning remains available.
- Focused unit/runtime coverage, fast-check routing, and current vehicle/input/network documentation.

## Assumptions, limitations and explicitly unverified areas

- The vehicle remains a ray-supported simplified chassis. Native practice and network sweep trajectories are not bit-identical; observed recovery and damage rules are consistent, not numerically identical trajectories.
- The six face bits coalesce continuing terrain contact. Distinct faces can produce distinct impacts while tumbling; generic wall/vehicle collision cooldown behavior was deliberately retained.
- Tests use production default tuning. Developer overrides can disable assistance or change the delay/rate; recovery is not guaranteed when explicitly disabled.
- Runtime playtests use deterministic pilots/controlled initial impacts. Physical-controller ergonomics, a human's subjective GTA V comparison, arbitrary map crevices, long competitive sessions and separate-device/Internet/EOS crash behavior are UNVERIFIED.
- Local impaired UDP, reconnect and migration results do not establish WAN/NAT or real EOS identity behavior. The stationary-roof scenario correctly causes no damage when contact severity remains below threshold.
- No unresolved requirement defect was identified in the completed implementation verification. Formal review may identify further issues.
- The pre-existing local change to `assets/maps/oval/grass_variation.png.import` is outside this Story and is excluded from delivery.
