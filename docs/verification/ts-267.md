### Reproduction and root cause

**VERIFIED performance defect:** original rock profiling isolated native `PhysicsServer3D.BodyTestMotion` at approximately 14–31 ms per sweep in contact. Support/ray work and Core stepping were much smaller. A matched RockCluster glancing probe at 12 m/s dropped from 13.829 ms to 2.675 ms average observation cost after simplification. See [original probe](ts-267-evidence/ts-267-probe-glancing-baseline.log) and [simplified probe](ts-267-evidence/ts-267-simplify.log).

Loading the saved original imported RockCluster into the same UDP harness, without editing source assets, produced two-world mean costs of 17.582–23.778 ms and p95 costs of 47.107–77.894 ms over six trials. See [original-hull UDP evidence](ts-267-evidence/ts-267-original-hulls-network.log). This supports excessive convex collision work as the cause of the reproduced slowdown.

The final p95 regression gate was also run against the original resource: it correctly failed the first trial at 48.419 ms p95 (17.321 ms mean). See [expected baseline failure](ts-267-evidence/ts-267-original-hulls-gate.log). Latest `origin/main` was fetched before review; local `main` remained identical, with zero ahead/behind commits.

**UNVERIFIED trapping cause:** original hulls also separated in these trials. Persistent embedding/trapping was not reproduced despite direct, glancing, slope, small-rock and repeated-contact probes. Reduced complexity does not prove every reported trapping condition fixed.
