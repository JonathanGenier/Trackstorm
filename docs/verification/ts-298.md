## Implemented result

- Core consumes the existing authoritative physical contact and retains the exact
  vehicle/life-local position and normal. Every claw has its own identity and
  original 1,800-tick deadline. New hits neither refresh earlier claws nor multiply
  their penalty.
- Existing vehicle movement scales engine/reverse drive and ground/air Nitro
  thrust to 20%. The final expiry/removal restores power at that fixed boundary.
  Steering, service braking, health, external impulses and existing momentum keep
  their ordinary rules. Tire traction still bounds realized acceleration.
- Complete item publications, fresh-admission checkpoints, reconnect checkpoints,
  authority recovery and movement prediction carry the appropriate membership or
  deadline. Target death/new life, explicit reset, final departure and match finish
  clear attachments. Transient suspension keeps original timers advancing.
- Production presentation follows the same rendered vehicle transform as the car:
  four-jaw grip, impact sparks, inflated red nose, local electricity and a shared
  vehicle effect. Each natural expiry deflates, fizzles, releases and falls away.
  Attached/in-flight EMP identities and cosmetic tails are bounded; the shared
  vehicle effect uses 96 core and 96 halo segments regardless of claw count.
- `RemoveEmpAttachment(id)` is a host-only, idempotent queued-removal seam for a
  future caller. No nose collision/damage, shield counterplay or stored-ammunition
  destruction was implemented. Standard Missile behavior was not changed.
- Item protocol TI30, movement version 14 (177 bytes), and discovery bucket 22
  reject incompatible layouts. Story versioning, workflow policies, documentation
  indexes, assets and dependencies were left unchanged as requested.

### Floating contacts: root causes and correction

**VERIFIED:** The movement chassis is a convex hull whose roof-height corners span
the hood. Eighteen vertical probes over the hood/window/roof measured as much as
**0.50865173 m** between that hull and authored visible bodywork. A valid hull hit
could therefore attach a claw in empty air. A second mismatch used the previous
native proxy pose for the hit but the newly solved observation for Core's local
conversion; moving/rotating impacts could gain an additional offset.

The host now uses a dedicated EMP sweep against a cached native triangle BVH of
the existing rigid vehicle mesh. It uses the same solved observations as Core,
preserves nearest world/prop obstruction and owner exclusion, and supplies the
actual point/normal to attachment authority. No predetermined mount or client
correction moves the accepted anchor. Movement and other weapons retain their
queries. Animated wheel/suspension/shock, trunk-hinge and weapon-rack assemblies
are deliberately excluded; rays can continue to rigid bodywork behind them. The
surface is constructed once per host arena and disposed with that arena.

Short seeded flight segments into the hood edge, windshield and a translating,
rotating hood produced real production contacts, authority transitions and native
replication. Every accepted anchor was within **0.002 m** of its contacted triangle.
The moving hood sample was local `(0.5095631, 0.47497135, -1.588275)` with normal
`(0.040731844, 0.99900824, -0.017982174)`. Six additional rotated front/rear/side/
roof/underside flights passed. The final 180-frame moving/rolling follow fixture
measured maximum rendered-anchor error **0.000017357544 m**. These are measured
fixture bounds, not guarantees for every possible contact or camera angle.
