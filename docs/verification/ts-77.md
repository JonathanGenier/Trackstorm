## Assumptions, limitations and risks

- Water currently shades/identifies the unchanged basin bed. Water plane/volume/depth classification remain later work.
- Identity is local observation, not replicated gameplay authority. Future handling/water consumers must integrate identity into authoritative contracts; existing handling/snapshot protocols are unchanged.
- Mixed wheel contacts select one deterministic material. Center wins; otherwise last supported wheel in FL/FR/RL/RR order wins.
- Lossless non-mipmapped 1024x512 field has 0.5 m texel spacing and bilinear interpolation. Texture detail does not affect identity. Cached fields require runtime restart after authoring edits.
- Existing stationary uphill-start behavior remains unresolved: the complete infield suite is **not green**. Successful driven transitions do not prove recovery from every stationary slope.
- Existing registered grass, Asphalt 04 and Concrete assets reused; no third-party acquisitions. Jira concept is not redistributed.
- Pre-existing edits to `InputButtons.cs` and `grass_variation.png.import` preserved and excluded from delivery.
