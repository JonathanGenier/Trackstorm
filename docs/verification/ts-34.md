# TS-34 / TS-35 arena audio verification

Verified on 2026-09-16 on Windows with Godot 4.7.2 .NET and .NET 10.
Delivery is on `ts-34-pg`; TS-35 has no separate branch or PR. The Story PR
remains subject to the workflow's human acceptance after critique.

## Implementation and requirement traceability

- `code/Client/Audio` owns native playback, the explicit clip catalog, bus routing,
  pure gain/cooldown/engine helpers, event projection and arena playlist.
- `NetworkVehicleArena` consumes confirmed lifecycle, item, match and vehicle
  boundaries; `VehicleArena` supplies practice vehicle feedback and its existing
  local blast. The previous synthetic item and vehicle sounds were removed.
- Vehicle idle/low/high loops crossfade continuously; grounded drift drives skid.
  Confirmed damage selects collision/heavy collision and damage feedback.
  Destruction adds a metal layer; local death and respawn have separate cues.
- Existing missile outcomes drive fire/impact/explosion; active missiles own travel
  loops. Grant tokens distinguish weapon/Wrench pickups; Wrench use works even at
  full health. Scored kills, countdown, Active and Finished drive personal/match
  cues. Arena ambience and a layered end sting complete the SFX package.
- Existing settings own Master/Music/SFX gains. Music and SFX send to Master;
  Vehicle, Weapons and UI send to SFX. No audio-manager plugin or new volume system.
- A Client selector randomizes only the starting song. Native completion advances
  sequentially and wraps 3 to 1. Leaving Active or freeing the arena stops/resets
  music. Duplicate Active publications do not restart it; reconnect seeds history.
- Core and authoritative payloads are unchanged. No Shared layer was introduced.
  Main-menu scenes, UI and audio behavior were untouched.

Current behavior and design limits are documented in [arena audio](../features/audio.md).
Affected vehicle, item, pickup, death/respawn, match, settings and reconnect feature
pages link that document.

## Verification limits

- **UNVERIFIED:** subjective listening, perceived mix balance, seamlessness to a
  listener, distinctness under a busy combat mix, speaker/headphone output and
  separate-machine authenticated EOS audio. Headless native playback and numerical
  gain tests do not establish those listening qualities.
- **VERIFIED:** continuous crossfade math, selected stream differences, native
  playback lifecycle and automated event deduplication. **INFERRED:** these choices
  provide appropriate prototype feedback; listening is still needed to judge feel.
- Engine layers use processed windows of a slow-driving recording, not calibrated
  RPM recordings. Gravel skid is shared across prototype surfaces. Road speed is
  the available presentation input; no authoritative engine/transmission was added.
- Collision cues follow confirmed damaging collisions. Harmless contacts have no
  new authoritative event and do not trigger a new audio-only physics rule.
- Practice has no match phase and therefore no battle playlist/countdown. Network
  Active matches own the playlist. Forty concurrent one-shot voices bound bursts.
- Fresh checkouts require the documented local Sonniss import before arena tests,
  startup or export. This is the explicitly approved delivery constraint.
