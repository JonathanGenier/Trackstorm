### Finding 1 — body/fender surface continuity

- **Problem:** the quarter panels connect structurally, but their broad segmented crowns, abrupt vertical end plates and sharp bonnet/quarter transitions still look assembled rather than like the reference car's more continuous formed bodywork.
- **Evidence:** final `front-closed.png`, `suspension-front.png` and `top-deployed.png`; the silhouette breaks are conspicuous at the front corners and upper arch shoulders.
- **Severity:** High for the production visual acceptance criterion.
- **Impact:** the vehicle retains a prototype-like surface treatment despite its recognizable car silhouette and stronger stance.
- **Suggested Improvement:** manually refine the existing Blender quarter/bonnet boundary loops, panel termination shapes and bevel/normal transitions against the front, side and high reference views, retaining the verified tire travel envelope.
- **Scope:** In Scope. **Corrective Work Type:** Existing Task (TS-164 Story asset work).

### Finding 2 — material and mechanical readability

- **Problem:** broad paint chips remain repetitive across panels; flat dark inner tubs compete with the thin shock details, so the twin-shock construction is much less readable at gameplay distance than in the low inspection view.
- **Evidence:** final front/low-front views and production-map turn capture; the rear lift reads more clearly than the wheel suspension.
- **Severity:** Medium.
- **Impact:** surface wear and mechanical depth do not yet match the intentional detail hierarchy of the references.
- **Suggested Improvement:** refine panel-specific wear distribution and material contrast, then adjust existing inner-well/shock visual separation in Blender while preserving pivots, travel and clearance. Assess from the normal gameplay camera, not only a close inspection angle.
- **Scope:** In Scope. **Corrective Work Type:** Existing Task (TS-164 Story asset work).

**Runtime/operational limitations:** sampled captures and bounded input provide no continuous human playtest judgment; exhaustive articulation collision, exact recovered animation phase, real EOS/internet peers, exported builds and profiled performance remain unverified as detailed above. Rapid authoritative use can precede the cosmetic rack cue; the sequence catches up without changing gameplay timing. These limits are not concealed by the passing checks.
