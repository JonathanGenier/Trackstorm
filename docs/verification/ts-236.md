## Implemented

- Replaced the ordinary orange GPU spark-emitter path with one dedicated
  `MachineGunImpactPresentation` MultiMesh. An original procedural shader draws
  angular two-tone chips, neutral dust and fine sparks without contact flames,
  fireballs, lights, scorch decals or explosive expansion.
- Metal/vehicle hits: six metallic flecks and two restrained sparks, no dust.
  Asphalt/Concrete/Rock: four chips, one spark and three dust wisps.
  Dirt/Grass/unknown: four chips and four dust wisps, no sparks.
  Mud/Deep Mud: four darker fragments; Water: four smaller pale flecks.
- Reused existing authored surface identities/fields through
  `SurfaceIdentityResolver`. A local probe spans ±0.6 m around the accepted
  endpoint to select normal/material; it never relocates the effect or decides
  damage. Unavailable local geometry uses neutral debris opposite the incoming ray.
- Fixed 1,024 instances: at most 128 bursts, 16 per shooter, eight particles per
  burst. Contacts coalesce per shooter within 25 ms and 0.6 m before sampling.
  Saturation drops cosmetics without replay. Lifetime is at most 0.42 s; sparks
  last 0.10–0.18 s and dust 0.24–0.36 s. No per-hit nodes, meshes, materials,
  emitters or physical debris are created. Projected chip sizing improves visibility.
- Release drains effects; duplicate publications do not replay; recovery and
  resynchronization clear the pool; arena teardown owns it. Shader is preloaded.
- Core, protocol, shot endpoints, damage, ammunition, tracer colors/sequencing
  and muzzle behavior are unchanged. No external assets or dependencies added.
- Added rendered impact checks, fast-check routing/regression coverage, and
  updated native assertions to observe the new pool rather than removed GPU nodes.
  Current feature and original-shader provenance documentation were updated.

## Assumptions and limitations

- Material and normals remain local cosmetics, not proof of applied damage.
  Moving/late peers can miss the small probe and display neutral fragments or
  choose different nearby materials. Accepted endpoints and damage remain authoritative.
- Flecks are camera-facing cosmetic geometry, not colliding debris. Water uses
  pale flecks at existing classified contacts; no new waterline or fluid simulation.
- Uniform surface identities were directly sampled; blended terrain uses the
  unchanged resolver. Exhaustive field boundaries and every imported prop were not tested.

## Unresolved risks

  explicitly requested no version change. Local passes do not establish CI or merge readiness.
- Human impact detection at high driving speed and worst-case overlap when many

## Human playtest setup

Start practice or a game session. In host Developer Options (F1), enable the
Weapons category, set Machine Gun pickup weight positive and every other item
weight to zero, then apply. Drive through a normal pickup. Tap/hold LMB and use
RMB aiming to compare nearby/farther cars, asphalt, concrete and infield surfaces.
Test while driving, then with multiple players and overlapping sustained fire.
Release, switch, deplete and reacquire to judge cleanup and readability. Use the
production 80 rounds/s and 800-round defaults for the primary assessment.
