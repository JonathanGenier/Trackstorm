## Implemented and reproduced causes

- **Rock landings/high-centering:** intact rocks were rejected by downward
  suspension queries, while upward chassis/rock contacts bypassed the existing
  inelastic lever-arm response. A truck could remain perched with unloaded tires.
  Downward rays now accept rock support, upward rock/chassis contacts use the
  existing impulse solver with chassis friction, and genuinely stranded rock
  contact uses the existing delayed physical roll recovery. Negligible tire droop
  contact no longer repeatedly cancels that recovery. Side anti-climb response,
  impact damage and ordinary driving remain in their existing owners.
- **Repeated rear contact:** the host's paired solve was smooth, but client
  prediction replay queried frozen remote poses between 20-Hz publications.
  Remote collision proxies now extrapolate to the queried local tick using
  published velocity within the existing 24-step prediction horizon, then restore
  accepted poses. Native transforms are explicitly flushed before the query;
  extrapolation without that flush did not fix the reproduction. Authority and
  presentation remain separate. A subsequent rendered check exposed visible
  interpenetration from the delayed remote render timeline. Nearby cars now use
  the same bounded estimate and local visible offset, blending continuously back
  to buffered rendering with distance. No collision cooldown was added.
- **Debris lifetime:** first fracture captures one authoritative deadline at
  destruction tick + 600. Descendants inherit it; further hits cannot prolong it.
  Expiry clears slots before impact processing and removes native visuals, wheel
  supports and weapon targets. TD version 3 carries the deadline through live
  publications and existing admission/resume/migration envelopes. Naturally small
  authored stones are not destroyed debris and do not time out.
- Added native repeated-contact and four-peer following checks, extended the
  imported-rock fixture for slow/close/embedded/drop scenarios, and added Core
  expiry/recovery coverage. Corrected stale map-count assertions to the existing
  authored 157 roots/628 reserved slots/2,288 plants; map assets were not changed.
  Updated affected feature documentation and fast-check routing.

## Assumptions, risks and explicitly unverified areas

- Ten seconds means 600 accepted fixed simulation ticks. Client display observes
  expiry through the existing publication cadence and transport delay; it does not
  introduce a second wall-clock destruction authority.
- Same-version peers are required: the environment wire version changed to TD3.
  Mixed-build compatibility is not provided or claimed.
- Remote query extrapolation is bounded constant velocity/angular velocity; it
  cannot predict another player's future braking, input or static-world impacts.
  Authoritative correction remains necessary during abrupt changes.
  Close-contact presentation shares this limit; distant vehicles still hold the
  endpoint of buffered snapshots.
- Native recovery/landing observations include physical roll and short transient
  penetration; the requirement is stable recovery, not zero impact motion.
- Authenticated EOS/Internet sessions, independent physical devices, exported
  builds, physical controllers, subjective audio evaluation and long-duration
  production soak beyond the recorded fixtures are explicitly unverified.
- The intermediate Godot resource-handle diagnostic above remains an intermittent
  runtime risk. The independent Astra run reproduced it on the completed build;
  see the formal critique below. Its causal relationship to TS-280 is unproven,
  and no claim of an unrelated loader fix is made.
