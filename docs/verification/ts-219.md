## Scope and implementation

Branch `ts-219-jg`, based on `origin/main` `e54eba1530d968c1f59c446b595ec3d3621b4f15`.

The user rejected the first literal tombstone design, selected Dozer + Ironclad,

preserve that approved design and the matching physical coverage. This supersedes
the earlier pending collider decision and the original literal tombstone art.

- Editable Blender 5.2.2 master, authoring script, audit, GLB shield and rack carriage.
  The unchanged production Car remains linked in the source as fit reference.
- Crimson/ivory armor, painted eyes/grin, welded chevron, brass stars, hinges and
  caged amber lamps. Three rigid full-height panels; wings rotate 90 degrees from
  wrapped rear sides to a 6.6 m-wide deployed wall over 0.42 seconds.
- Release interpolates from the displayed vehicle pose over 0.18 seconds. Car
  sockets, trunk/rack path and wheel articulation remain the production mechanisms.
- Core/native coverage now uses three matching panel boxes; the default deployed
  width is 6.6 m. Release clearance omits only the armor consumed by deployment,
  while retaining chassis/other-vehicle/world clearance. No HP, inventory authority,
  protocol, mass, lifetime, damage law or vehicle force-law changes.
- Accepted HP drives per-identity scars, flash/sparks and lamp dimming. Non-colliding
  breakup lasts 0.8 seconds, capped at 32 world effects / four per vehicle. Stored
  identities retain damage; recovery installs accepted state without replaying use.
- Two-peer command-driven runtime fixture and a targeted presentation check.

pose and damage commands are intentional fixture controls; selection/use/driving
use ordinary input frames. This is actual rendered execution, not a Blender render
or a claim of physical keyboard/controller play. Native shield/wall harnesses also
exercise weapon and vehicle hits through the real adapters.

destruction, simultaneous-wall and chase-camera captures from

## Iteration defects and corrections

- The first design's rear-guard overlap and noisy surface were corrected before
  it was superseded by the user-approved design. Its passing checks are historical,
  not final evidence for the new geometry.
- Early Carnage Circus passes exposed a small brace/Car overlap and then insufficient
  clearance. Moving the mount aft and narrowing the inner brace produced the final
  4.46 cm conservative clearance. Incorrect color-space conversion and excessive
  shader speckling were corrected after rendered inspection.
- Production-map scenario 5 failed release clearance on a slope. Diagnostic queries
  identified the deploying Car's own new armor panels as the obstruction. Placement
  now temporarily excludes only those consumed panels; all sixteen scenarios and
  the blocked-use/retry assertion then passed. Diagnostic code was removed.
- Destruction initially disposed materials during reparenting. Disposal now occurs
  on native deletion; subsequent breakup and cleanup checks passed without exceptions.
- File replacement raced the fixture reader; shared-delete reads and atomic input
  replacement corrected the command handoff.
- Rack checks had stale pre-TS-218 Tombstone expectations (closed rack/rejected use).
  They now exercise the real carriage and existing normal deployment.
- General item checks used obsolete controller D-pad Right/A bindings; they now use
  production X/Y. The old 1.6 m prop sat below the production missile trajectory at
  approximately 1.98 m. The fixture now uses a matching rendered/physical 3 m movable
  target and retains the impulse assertion. Production missile behavior is unchanged.
- Two earlier general impaired-network runs failed the existing 0.25 m steady
  correction P99 gate: 0.3682603 m under concurrent build load and 0.3409142 m in
  isolation (max 0.8585650 m, zero hard snaps). These predate the final approved asset;
  no thresholds were changed. Final results/baseline limitations must remain explicit.

The final impaired run passed without concurrent verification load, but a clean-main
comparison was not run and the cause of the earlier variability remains unproven.

## Assumptions, risks and unverified areas

- Conservative panel/wall boxes own collision; small bevels, lamps, crown trim and
  painted cutouts are cosmetic. The full wall collider activates immediately during
  the cosmetic 0.42-second expansion, preserving existing authoritative timing.
- Full-height mounted coverage and 6.6 m deployed width are intentional approved
  changes to the previous temporary protection envelope; competitive balance is unverified.
- Damage marks represent remaining HP, not exact hit positions. Expiry uses the same
  breakup as destruction. No dedicated Tombstone audio or final HUD icon was added.
- Independent-device EOS/WAN, physical-controller ergonomics and other GPUs/platforms
  have not been exercised. Local native UDP is not evidence for those environments.
